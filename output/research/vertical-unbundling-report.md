# Vertical Unbundling Opportunities Beyond Skilved

The strongest opportunity is to take one expensive, recurring business outcome out of an improvised combination of messaging, documents, spreadsheets and broad software, then make that outcome reliable. For a South African founder with global venture ambition, the best initial search is around **commercial evidence and exception resolution**: establishing what was ordered, delivered, approved, disputed and ultimately payable.

This report evaluates twelve entry theses across eleven fields. The recommended discovery priorities are **wholesale claims and deductions**, **specialist contractor payment evidence**, and, with a domain partner, **export shipment evidence coordination**. Contractor procurement is a closely related alternative. These are ranked experiments, not validated investment opportunities. Every leading candidate has direct competitors; the possible opening is a specific underserved customer and workflow, rather than an entirely new category.

### Recommended decisions

| Priority | First customer and outcome | Investment decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A repeat-order distributor resolves shortages, returns and short payments | Run a paid retrospective and live claims pilot |
| 2 | A specialist subcontractor assembles accepted payment applications | Compare actual cases with Clearstory before building |
| 3 | An exporter assembles a complete, consistent shipment dossier | Proceed only with an anchor exporter and domain partner |
| Alternative | A commercial HVAC contractor resolves missing parts and supplier commitments | Test if procurement access is stronger than finance access |

The ordering reflects proximity to measurable economic value and the possibility of adoption by one paying organisation. It does not imply that the first market is the least competitive. A trusted introduction to five suitable contractors could make the second option a better first experiment than the first.

### What the playbook actually requires

A narrow product becomes strategically valuable when it completes a job, becomes the trusted record for that job, and earns access to adjacent recurring work. It can then expand from claims into settlement, from payment evidence into contractor commercial operations, or from shipment evidence into cross-border trade operations. This expansion must be earned through customer use and distribution.

Yoco provides a relevant South African illustration: the company describes moving from easier payment acceptance into capital and point-of-sale tools. That is evidence of an actual product expansion path, not proof that a new entrant should copy payments or that its reported merchant outcomes are causal.[^5]

The analysis is current to **14 September 2026** and assumes a South Africa first launch with global ambition, without assuming funding or industry access already exists. Public evidence establishes market context and competing offers. Proposed prices, customer profiles, rankings and pilot thresholds are explicitly analytical assumptions. No customer interviews, paid tests or independent product demonstrations have yet established demand for the proposed gaps.

## The strategy behind the analogy

The WhatsApp example is useful as a functional comparison, but its literal history is overstated. Meta's 2014 acquisition announcement describes an independently built cross-platform mobile messaging company. It does not establish that WhatsApp's founders set out to extract Facebook messaging.[^1] Airbnb's IPO filing describes hosting guests in 2007 and designing trust between strangers; Tinder's own history places its beginning on a university campus in 2012. Neither establishes a simple deliberate extraction from Craigslist.[^2][^3]

The distinction matters because a copied feature does not inherit the original platform's customers, trust, distribution or economics. The 2019 a16z essay on platforms and verticals describes specialisation around a category and a more suitable experience or business model. It is a useful strategic argument, not a law of startup success.[^4]

| Strategic move | Meaning | Applied example |
|---|---|---|
| Functional unbundling | Separate one job from a broader tool | Turn scattered payment evidence into a dedicated workflow |
| Verticalisation | Tailor that job to one industry and buyer | Handle a specialist contractor's submission requirements |
| Rebundling | Add adjacent work around the same record | Connect approvals, retention, procurement and margin |

### A complete narrow outcome

The starting product should carry a customer from a clear trigger to a useful result. For example, a distributor receives a deduction, gathers the right delivery evidence, assigns a decision owner, and closes the discrepancy in accounting. A dashboard showing that the documents are missing is only part of that job.

The original bundle may be organisational rather than a single platform. A finance administrator, shared drive, WhatsApp group and accounting package collectively provide today's service. Replacing part of this arrangement is worthwhile only if the product removes more coordination than it introduces.

### Three distinct forms of advantage

First, a **workflow advantage** means the narrow task is completed materially faster or more reliably. Second, a **distribution advantage** means the founder can reach a specific group economically through a practitioner, supplier or existing network. Third, an **expansion advantage** means the same transaction record supports additional paid work. An attractive venture normally needs evidence for all three over time.

Network effects should be claimed carefully. A growing archive within one customer's account can improve retention, but is not automatically a network. A network effect appears when additional participants make the service more useful to existing participants: for example, a distributor and its carrier resolving claims through a mutually accepted record. Access permissions and actual counterparty adoption are prerequisites.

## South Africa as an entry market

South Africa can be a useful place to learn a narrow workflow, secure references and develop implementation expertise. It should not be treated as a cheap copy of the United States, or as evidence of a uniform African market. Local commercial relationships, budgets, accounting systems and institutional rules must shape the first product.

Stats SA's 2024 construction survey covers VAT-registered enterprises. Its July 2026 summary reports that small and micro enterprises accounted for 32.2% of construction income and 55.3% of employment in 2024; activity was concentrated in Gauteng, Western Cape and KwaZulu-Natal. The same analysis describes a shrinking real construction economy. Fragmentation creates potential customers, while weak sector conditions constrain budgets and increase churn risk.[^6]

Government's July 2024 statement on supplier payments documents serious public-sector delays in 2023/24. This supports the importance of cash flow, but does not identify how much delay came from documentation. A product cannot solve deliberate non-payment, insolvency or procurement politics merely by assembling a cleaner invoice.[^7]

### Entry principles

Start in the metro where the founder can repeatedly meet suitable buyers. Choose one industry subtype, one economic buyer, and initially one accounting or operational system. “All South African SMEs” is too broad to define the product, its price or a repeatable sales process.

Prefer an existing business relationship over a new marketplace. One distributor can adopt software using its existing customers and carriers. One contractor can use a payment pack with its existing main contractors. This allows the first customer to benefit before a new network reaches scale.

Use messaging as an input and notification channel. Keep the authoritative record accessible through a browser and exportable files. Buyers and counterparties should not have to abandon familiar communication before experiencing value. Low-data forms, explicit offline status and recovery from interrupted uploads are useful design hypotheses to test with actual users.

### Screening method

The ranking weighs five qualitative questions: Is the loss or effort measurable? Does a named buyer control a budget? Can one organisation adopt independently? Can the founder reach a concentrated customer group? Can adjacent workflows support a larger business? Competitor strength and implementation cost can override an otherwise attractive score.

Evidence confidence is **high for the existence of competing offers**, **moderate for broad problem relevance**, and **low for unmet demand in the exact proposed segments**. No numeric score can repair missing buyer evidence. The leading ideas deserve discovery because their assumptions can be tested with real records; they do not yet deserve a large product build.

## Wholesale claims and deductions

**First choice for a commercial validation experiment.** Start with independent electrical, building-supply or packaging distributors serving repeat business accounts. Select one subtype after obtaining access to real cases. The economic buyer is the finance or credit-control manager; warehouse staff, salespeople and carriers supply evidence.

### The job to separate

A customer pays less than invoiced because it claims a shortage, damaged goods, an incorrect price or an unprocessed return. The finance team must reconstruct events across delivery notes, photos, orders, emails, credits and remittances. The proposed product carries that case from receipt to an authorised decision and accounting closure.

Esker already advertises this category, including deductions associated with shortages, damage and price discrepancies, document matching, approvals and ERP reconciliation. HighRadius also markets deductions automation, including to the mid-market. Consequently, “AI for deductions” is already a competing offer, and “enterprise tools ignore smaller customers” is an assumption requiring a real comparison.[^8][^9]

### The opening to investigate

Test whether a specific South African supplier group has recurring claims that existing tools cannot handle economically with its current accounting system and buyer requirements. The potential edge is reliable implementation of that exact workflow, supported by an accounting or ERP channel. Local currency and cheaper OCR are not sufficient differentiation.

Use an existing delivery record wherever possible. PODsync already offers local delivery and returns features, with advertised standard tiers from R390 to R690 monthly. Replacing a driver's existing app creates a second adoption problem before proving value in finance.[^10]

### First product

Create a case record containing the customer, invoice, disputed line, amount, reason, evidence, responsible person and next action. Link source documents at field level; retain uncertainty when an invoice reference or quantity cannot be matched. Generate a buyer-specific response pack, then record whether the claim was valid, rejected, settled or credited.

Humans approve commercial decisions and accounting changes. A successful product must also identify legitimate deductions quickly. Maximising rejection or “money recovered” at the expense of accuracy can damage customer relationships and misstate value.

### Distribution and pricing hypothesis

Recruit through an ERP implementer or bookkeeper serving several similar distributors. Offer a paid diagnostic of an agreed historical sample, followed by a six-week operational pilot. Test **R3,000 to R12,000 per month** for the live service, with integration scope separately specified. This is a proposed price range, not observed willingness to pay. The buyer should be able to show recurring benefit comfortably exceeding the fee without speculative future financing revenue.

## Wholesale validation and expansion

### Evidence required before development

Obtain authorised access to around 100 historical discrepancy cases from each of three suitable firms, including resolved cases. A sample consisting only of difficult unresolved claims exaggerates the opportunity. Classify the cause of each case: missing evidence, wrong internal data, legitimate concession, buyer disagreement or inability to pay.

Reconstruct twenty cases manually for each firm. Establish how many could actually have reached a different or faster outcome. Measure handling minutes, time awaiting evidence, resolution days, valid credit amounts and previously unrecovered amounts. Track changes in cash collection separately; a six-week pilot may be too short to observe the full payment cycle.

The proposed gate is three paying pilots, at least a 30% reduction in median handling time, and evidence that the second and third firms can onboard using the same integration pattern. The exact thresholds are management choices. Every incorrect match or proposed credit should be reviewed as carefully as time savings.

### Why generic ordering is lower priority

Brand2Market's indexed product page already advertises South African WhatsApp ordering, integrations, AI product matching and human review. Its advertised fee is 0.3% of processed sales, with a R3,000 monthly minimum and R10,000 ceiling, excluding Meta messaging charges. Direct page extraction was unavailable, so these commercial terms require confirmation before use in a purchase decision.[^11]

Choco's July 2026 documentation explicitly handles forwarded WhatsApp messages, screenshots and voice notes. Orderspace advertises a B2B ordering product from GBP39 per month excluding VAT.[^12][^13] These examples make a generic catalogue-plus-chat ordering proposition hard to defend. Existing order products might become integrations or referral partners, but no such partnership should be presumed.

### Path to a larger company

Expand from one type of claim into multi-branch resolution, supplier returns, cash application and commercial settlement records. Only then test counterparty collaboration and payment-provider participation. Growth could come from more branches and cases per customer, additional modules, and replication of the same distributor subtype abroad.

The potential durable assets are the implemented workflow, integrations, documented resolution history and customer trust. Cross-company benchmarks require permission and appropriate aggregation. A proprietary archive does not justify selling confidential customer data or assuming unrestricted model-training rights.

Stop if evidence rarely changes outcomes, most claims are valid concessions, critical buyer data is inaccessible, or onboarding remains bespoke after three similar firms. Also stop if an incumbent's actual implementation completes the same cases at acceptable total cost. A useful diagnostic service is not automatically a scalable product, and recovered invoice principal is not software revenue.

## Specialist contractor payment evidence

**Second discovery priority, potentially first with strong founder access.** Target commercial electrical, HVAC or fit-out subcontractors with several active projects and recurring payment applications. The buyer is the owner or commercial manager; a foreman supplies evidence and a quantity surveyor or main contractor reviews it.

### The job to separate

An instruction changes the work. The contractor must capture the instruction, establish scope and price, document performance, obtain the appropriate review and include the supported amount in a payment application. The proposed entry product maintains a contractor-owned record across customers with different submission formats.

Current cidb roadshow remarks identify delayed payment as a threat to contractors. They provide qualitative evidence of pain, not a statistic showing that missing paperwork causes a particular share of delays.[^14] The initial value proposition should therefore be reduced preparation effort and fewer avoidable submission defects, with payment improvements measured separately.

### The strongest competitive objection

Clearstory already provides digital time-and-material tags, change-order requests, shared logs and a free Basic tier with a small monthly allowance. Its AI agent converts voice or text into tags using configured project information and rates. Free stakeholder participation and AI capture cannot be claimed as new advantages.[^15][^16]

Local JobCardHub advertises evidence capture and administration at R399 per month including one team, plus R99 per additional team.[^17] A new product priced materially above basic job cards must complete additional commercial work that buyers value.

The narrow residual hypothesis is **payment-application completeness for a specific subcontractor group**, including the customer's supporting-document requirements, approved versus disputed amounts, prior submissions and retention records. Start alongside existing job software. Contract interpretation and payment entitlement remain with authorised professionals; the product should not turn an inferred site instruction into an asserted legal claim.

### Initial product and pricing

Manually assemble a month's payment evidence for five firms before choosing features. The first software should contain a project register, original instruction, named participants, approval status, amount history, missing-evidence queue and export matching the customer's required format. A simple document-only workflow may be enough to test whether the buyer values the result.

Test **R1,500 to R5,000 per contractor per month**, with disclosed setup work where justified. Recruit through construction bookkeepers, quantity surveyors and specialist trade networks. The founder should sell a specific accepted output, such as a complete application pack, rather than an abstract “construction operating system”.

## Contractor validation and procurement

### A head to head pilot

Select five historical applications per contractor, including at least one rejected or amended submission. Separate administrative defects from disputes about entitlement, workmanship or commercial bargaining. Use the same evidence to compare the proposed workflow with Clearstory and the customer's existing process.

Clearstory's Accurate Firestop case reports faster processing and reduced aging. It is a named vendor case, not an independent causal study or a forecast for South Africa.[^18] Its relevance is that the value proposition already has a credible competitor, so interview enthusiasm alone is weak evidence of an opening.

The proposed six-week gate is three paying customers from five pilots, capture of at least 70% of eligible new events before the submission deadline, and a 30% reduction in preparation time without poorer evidence quality. A reviewer should confirm the pack is usable. A refusal to use a new portal may be solved by an export; refusal to acknowledge valid work may be a power problem software cannot solve.

Expansion can follow the commercial record into progress applications, retention, project margin and procurement. Test the same trade in a second country only after understanding its contracts and buyer process. A collection of national templates without repeatable distribution is more likely to remain a specialist service business.

### An alternative within the contractor market

Commercial refrigeration and HVAC contractors also coordinate parts across jobs, suppliers and vehicles. The proposed procurement product identifies an unconfirmed order, late promised delivery, short receipt or unexpected price and connects it to a job and responsible employee. It initially works through supplier emails and existing purchase orders.

This is also competed. Commusoft already links supplier purchase orders and stock to job costs. Tradecloud's published buyer Starter package is EUR998 monthly, billed annually, plus implementation and other stated charges.[^19][^20] An enterprise price point does not prove that a South African contractor will buy a smaller product.

The entry test is a paid reconstruction of one week's open orders at five contractors. Seek a recurring failure that existing system configuration does not solve. Test **R5,000 to R15,000 monthly** only where prevented delay or removed labour supports it. Qualified staff must authorise equipment compatibility and substitutions.

Choose payment evidence or procurement for the first build. They share potential customers, but have different users and integrations. Procurement could eventually connect installed assets, suppliers and replenishment; payment evidence could become a contractor commercial platform. Starting with both hides which product customers actually need and doubles the adoption burden.

## Export shipment evidence coordination

**Third priority, conditional on a domain partner and an anchor exporter.** Start with one commodity and corridor. A possible first investigation is the evidence handoff between a South African exporter, supplier and forwarding desk. The exact commodity should be chosen through access to real shipment packs, not merely the size of exports.

### The job to separate

Collect the necessary evidence, identify inconsistent references or quantities, assign missing items to an owner, track revisions and produce an accepted shipment dossier. Focus on work surrounding official and incumbent systems, especially evidence that must arrive from counterparties before a specialist can submit or approve it.

South Africa already provides electronic phytosanitary certification. eCert publishes an API requiring client registration, and its documented application endpoint describes a fruit-specific scope. These facts establish existing infrastructure and access constraints; they do not establish that a startup has production API access or that all commodities are supported.[^21][^22]

Paltrack already serves exporters and agents, while CargoWise markets customs and compliance automation. IncoDocs displays document-generation plans including USD29 and USD74 monthly options, with billing selections requiring confirmation.[^23][^24][^25] A generic shipping-document generator would face both specialist depth and inexpensive alternatives.

### First product

Build a shipment record containing the identifiers, parties, quantities, original documents, revisions and approval history relevant to the chosen workflow. Show the source of each extracted field. Flag mismatches for review instead of silently rewriting a certificate or treating plausible text as verified.

The responsible export professional defines and approves the dossier. Applicable requirements vary with the goods and route; the initial product should preserve the rule's jurisdiction, version and effective date. The customer must be able to explain where a requirement came from and who approved a change.

### Buyer and acquisition

The buyer may be an exporter operations manager or specialised forwarding desk. Select the party that both experiences the delay and controls a budget. A knowledgeable export practitioner is necessary to define the first workflow and introduce firms with similar shipments.

Test three organisations using the same corridor before supporting another commodity. Charge a proposed **R5,000 to R20,000 per month**, or a transparent per-shipment fee where frequency is seasonal. The first commercial claim is faster preparation and fewer preventable corrections. Port congestion, inspection outcomes and vessel schedules should not be counted as benefits unless the pilot establishes a causal link.

## Export validation and platform limits

### The first evidence test

Review thirty completed shipment packs with a domain practitioner. Include successful shipments and corrected ones. Record the missing document, mismatch, originating party, detection point, staff handling time and actual consequence. Distinguish a late supplier response from an avoidable internal failure, and a document problem from a physical logistics delay.

Run the proposed workflow prospectively on a new cohort while keeping official submissions under existing controls. Suggested gates are two or three paying organisations, a 30% reduction in preparation effort, no unreviewed critical changes, and acceptance of the exported pack by the relevant downstream professional. A quiet off-season month is insufficient evidence of retention or recurring volume.

Kill the idea if the same work is already handled by the incumbent, if source evidence cannot be accessed lawfully and reliably, or if each shipment requires fresh consultancy. Also reject a product that merely reports missing documents while staff still do all the chasing and reconciliation elsewhere.

### Integration is part of the business model

SARS describes an established electronic data interchange process with registration, software and technical requirements. Product plans must distinguish preparing a pack from submitting a declaration.[^26] Begin with authorised exports and handovers where useful; add production integrations after confirming access and repeatable demand.

API availability is not equivalent to customer permission, contractual rights or complete data coverage. Budget for onboarding, credential management, changing schemas and manual fallbacks. A product that becomes uneconomic when a portal changes is a fragile business even if its demo is convincing.

### Expansion that could support venture scale

The common unit is the shipment and its verified evidence. Expand first to another customer with the same workflow, then another corridor with the same commodity, and only later adjacent commodities. Collaboration can widen to freight milestones and receivables evidence if the same records are repeatedly useful.

Global expansion is plausible when the transaction model stays stable and local requirements can be maintained through limited configuration and accountable partners. It is weak when every country requires a separate product and advisory team. International trade volume is not a software market estimate; the relevant market consists of organisations with enough recurring addressable work and budget to buy.

Payments and working-capital partnerships are possible extensions, but should not rescue weak subscription economics in the initial thesis. The company must first earn trust in its evidence. Holding customer funds, underwriting credit or guaranteeing release introduces obligations and risks distinct from document coordination.

## Property fleet and production workflows

These fields contain real recurring coordination work, but should enter the shortlist only when customer access reveals a more precise gap than the broad product category.

### Rental maintenance approvals and cost recovery

The candidate buyer is a property manager coordinating landlords, tenants and contractors across existing systems. A possible wedge is linking authorised scope, cost responsibility, before-and-after evidence, repeat warranty visits and invoice recovery. RedRabbit already connects requests, inspections and maintenance; Property Inspect advertises South African pricing tiers of R950, R1,950 and R4,950 monthly. Some published plan limits conflict and need confirmation.[^27][^28]

Test two managers' completed work orders for avoidable write-offs or duplicate administration. Only propose a pilot, for example R1,500 monthly minimum, when the retrospective identifies recurring value beyond existing configuration. Expansion into portfolio procurement and contractor settlement could enlarge the business. Generic maintenance tickets have little differentiated venture potential; replacing property accounting prematurely would add substantial migration risk.

### Fleet repair approval and readiness

The candidate buyer operates twenty to one hundred vehicles and already has tracking software. The possible wedge is defect report to workshop quote, approval, repair evidence and return-to-service status. Fleetio publishes annual-plan pricing of USD4, USD7 and USD10 per vehicle monthly, subject to bands and terms; Cartrack already offers maintenance scheduling and reminders.[^29][^30]

Test three fleets and their existing workshops. Measure the portion of downtime caused by approval administration, not all mechanical downtime. An account minimum plus R40 to R100 per vehicle monthly is a pricing experiment, not a market fact. A broader repair-authorisation network could become valuable, but reminders alone are easy to bundle. Qualified personnel remain responsible for roadworthiness and technical decisions.

### Print and packaging approval to production

The candidate buyer is a printer or packaging producer suffering disputes about which artwork, specification or revision was approved. The proposed outcome is a versioned production release linked to the commercial change. Ziflow already offers a free tier with unlimited proofs and reviewers and a USD199 monthly Standard plan billed annually.[^31]

Review ten reprints or disputed revisions, then test whether linking approval to production prevents measurable rework. A proposed R1,000 to R4,000 monthly pilot must outperform existing proofing tools plus a checklist. This can be a useful specialist software business. A venture thesis would require expansion into specifications, estimating, production and repeat purchasing, bringing materially more implementation and competitive complexity.

## Training care compliance and waste

### Workplace competency evidence

One occupation with distributed workplaces offers a possible capture-to-supervisor-approval workflow. QCTO's provider guidance establishes structured evidence and reporting requirements, while skillSYMS and Yiba Verified already advertise learner records, portfolios, logbooks and related administration.[^32][^33][^34] A generic training platform is therefore not a new opening.

Test three providers and one employer using the same qualification. Measure missing-evidence rework and whether an employer actually reuses accepted evidence across providers. Expansion into workforce eligibility and recurring skills records could enlarge the opportunity. A South African reporting utility alone is narrower. For Skilved, this is an adjacent hypothesis with potentially shared learning and contacts; it offers limited diversification from the existing career-evidence direction.

### Home care visit administration

Independent agencies could pay to connect visit completion, accountable handovers and invoices. Birdie already combines extensive agency operations, while local-facing Sikhaya Health advertises scheduling and case-management workflows.[^35][^36] Family messaging alone is a weak starting product.

Proceed with an experienced care operator and two paying agencies. Measure coordinator handling time and invoice reconciliation while monitoring missed escalations. The larger ambition is agency operations across countries, with substantial professional and data-governance work. This ranks below the commercial workflows because adoption failures can have consequences beyond wasted administrative time.

### SME compliance exceptions

The FIC's 2024/25 report records 556 risk-based inspections and 330 reports requiring remediation. This is not a representative non-compliance rate for all firms. nCino KYC Africa already sells local onboarding, screening, risk and compliance support.[^37][^38]

A consultant-to-client remediation queue for one industry is a possible wedge. Test through two compliance consultants, require paying firms and confirm that a second consultant can onboard without bespoke development. Cross-institution evidence reuse could support expansion, but the institution's risk decision and legal responsibility do not automatically transfer with a document.

### Waste traceability and producer reporting

DFFE's guidance describes registration for producers and producer-responsibility organisations within identified EPR streams. ATS already markets e-waste traceability and reporting, so this is also an occupied field.[^39][^40] Registration software alone is insufficient.

With a waste operator as a domain partner, investigate reconciliation of collection, weight and downstream treatment evidence for one material stream. An initial pilot must demonstrate trustworthy reconciliation without merely converting questionable records into digital ones. Expansion could support multiple producers and jurisdictions; collector incentives, verification costs and local rules make it more demanding than a reporting dashboard.

## Consumer and community opportunities

The unbundling strategy can work beyond B2B software. These candidates rank lower here because a new entrant must often establish consumer trust, repeated participation and transaction economics before a clear payer emerges. They become more attractive if the founder already has privileged access to a community.

### Trusted resale for a specialist category

The opportunity hypothesis is a transaction service for used musical equipment or another enthusiast category: condition inspection, serial-number evidence, agreed fulfilment and a defined dispute process. Start with repair shops and existing sellers, using a manual transaction service before developing a listing platform.

Bike Hub demonstrates an existing South African vertical model in cycling. Its Pay service holds funds until buyer acceptance and advertises a 3.9% protection fee, subject to minimum and maximum amounts.[^41] That supports the relevance of trust infrastructure; it does not establish demand in musical equipment or justify copying its financial arrangement without appropriate partners.

The first test is twenty completed transactions within a tightly defined community. Measure conversion, inspection and support costs, failed deliveries, disputes and contribution after refunds. Registrations are not liquidity. A marketplace must have the right supply when a buyer needs it and retain enough value to prevent routine off-platform transactions.

Expansion could follow professional seller inventory, trade-ins, servicing and category-specific valuation. However, every new geography requires its own supply density and reliable fulfilment. An existing audience or trusted inspection network would improve this thesis substantially; absent that, it is a weaker first bet than selling a defined workflow to an existing business.

### Activity coordination for parents and providers

A possible starting customer is an independent after-school provider coordinating permissions, attendance, transport arrangements and payment. Sell completion of one recurring activity workflow, and let providers invite their existing parents. Do not start by demanding that all schools adopt a new parent social network.

d6 already supports school communications through multiple channels, including WhatsApp, and offers parent payments. d6 Pay advertises transaction charges without setup or subscription fees.[^42][^43] A cleaner school noticeboard is insufficient differentiation.

Test three providers serving a specific activity category. Require payment from the business buyer and show less missing-permission or reconciliation work across a full activity cycle. Children and families introduce additional privacy and safeguarding requirements; these belong in product design, not as an afterthought to growth.

Expansion might connect several providers around a family's schedule, but competing incentives are a serious barrier: parents use the tool, providers may pay, and schools can mandate another system. This could be an attractive niche with strong distribution. Its venture potential remains conditional on repeatable provider acquisition and broader transaction value.

## Venture scale and economic discipline

Large vertical businesses exist, but their outcomes do not establish the probability of success for a new entrant. ServiceTitan reported USD961 million FY2026 revenue and approximately 10,800 active customers. Toast reported more than USD2 billion annualised recurring run-rate at December 2025; its measure includes adjusted payment-service fees and is not directly comparable with pure subscription ARR.[^44][^45]

nCino's FY2025 filing records a preliminary USD74.3 million purchase price for DocFox. This illustrates a specialist business becoming part of a broader platform; it is not evidence that every niche must become an independent billion-dollar company.[^46]

### Reverse sizing the ambition

The following arithmetic is a set of scale conditions, **not TAM, forecasts, valuations or exchange-rate conversions**. Customer counts and prices are hypothetical. Each row asks what the business would have to achieve.

| Scenario | Explicit calculation | Annual recurring revenue |
|---|---|---|
| Focused regional business | 1,000 customers x R2,000 x 12 | R24 million |
| Larger international business | 10,000 customers x R5,000 x 12 | R600 million |
| Illustrative global software hurdle | 10,000 customers x USD833.33 x 12 | About USD100 million |

A claim about a huge construction, wholesale or export economy cannot fill the gap between these rows. Build a named customer universe for the exact subtype; deduplicate branches and legal entities, then filter for transaction volume, systems, buyer budget and reachable distribution. No reliable count for the exact proposed South African niches has been established here.

### A pilot unit economics model

At a hypothetical R5,000 monthly subscription, R500 infrastructure and extraction cost plus R1,000 ongoing support produces R3,500 monthly gross contribution, or 70%. An R18,000 acquisition cost plus R8,000 unrecovered onboarding cost implies about 7.4 months to recover that investment from gross contribution. This excludes fixed engineering, management, tax and financing costs.

If recurring support rises to R2,500, contribution falls to R2,000 or 40%, and recovery takes thirteen months. This sensitivity is why “AI can process the documents” is insufficient: human exception work can dominate economics. Separate implementation revenue from subscriptions and include founder-delivered labour at a realistic replacement cost.

For transaction revenue, use completed volume multiplied by the **net retained fee**, then deduct provider charges, refunds, disputes and operational costs. Invoice value, payment volume and recovered principal are not revenue. Financing spreads require a separate model for funding cost, losses and compliance. Build the company around sustainable product value before adding balance-sheet risk.

## Distribution defensibility and product design

### Sell through a concentrated professional network

For deductions, recruit an ERP implementer with several similar distributors. For contractors, recruit a quantity surveyor or construction bookkeeper. For exports, recruit a corridor specialist. A channel is valuable only if it introduces qualified buyers and supports repeatable onboarding; a verbal promise of referrals is not distribution.

The first sales artifact should be a customer-specific example: an unresolved claim assembled into a decision pack, an application with missing evidence identified, or a shipment dossier reconciled against originals. Ask the buyer to compare it with the current process and an incumbent demonstration. Do not ask only whether the idea sounds useful.

### Product structure

Use five layers: capture original evidence; identify the transaction and parties; resolve exceptions through named owners; record approvals and downstream actions; export or integrate with the customer's system. This structure supports a complete job without requiring an immediate replacement of accounting, logistics or project software.

AI can extract candidate fields, classify a case, draft a request and propose a match. Deterministic checks should govern calculations, duplicate detection and allowed state changes. Material decisions need appropriate human approval. Preserve originals, corrections and provenance so a disputed result can be reconstructed.

### What could become defensible

The most credible advantages are repeated use, reliable integration, lower implementation cost within a segment, trusted distribution and accumulated knowledge of how cases are resolved. Measure whether each new customer becomes cheaper to onboard and whether the product handles a larger share of recurring work. Documents stored and model prompts written are weak measures of defensibility.

An incumbent can copy a feature or bundle it into an existing contract. A September 2026 a16z analysis explicitly discusses the threat from systems of record combined with general agents, while arguing that specialised execution can still matter. Treat this as an investor thesis rather than independent proof.[^51] The practical defence is to complete a particular job better and distribute it economically.

### Expansion gates

Add an adjacent module when the same buyer repeatedly requests it and the existing record removes substantial setup work. Add a country when an anchor customer or channel provides access and the workflow remains largely reusable. Add a network function when counterparties independently return to use it. Each is a new hypothesis with its own adoption and economics test.

The first international market should follow customer and partner access, not an automatic “Africa then everywhere” sequence. A matched distributor or contractor segment in an English-speaking market may be easier than a geographically closer country with different systems. Actual selection requires local customer and regulatory diligence.

## Platform and regulatory dependencies

These constraints materially affect the proposed business models. They are design and commercial questions to resolve with relevant practitioners, rather than a claim that every candidate needs the same regulatory treatment.

### Messaging dependency

WhatsApp's official pricing page describes delivered-message charges varying by market and category, with specified free service-window treatment. Model provider and messaging costs explicitly, and verify current rate cards before a live pilot. The public page should not be treated as a guarantee that today's pricing or access rules will persist.[^47]

Do not build the core product around covert access to personal chats or scraping private groups. Use business-authorised ingestion, deliberate forwarding and supported integrations. The customer must still be able to find records and complete work if a messaging integration is unavailable.

### Personal and confidential information

POPIA includes security safeguards, operator arrangements, breach obligations, special personal-information conditions and rules governing transfers abroad. Health-related products need particular attention; cross-border hosting is not equivalent to an unconditional ban or unconditional permission.[^49] Assign controller/responsible-party and operator roles correctly, limit collection, set retention, control access and document the chosen lawful processing basis.

The Information Regulator's direct-marketing guidance is relevant to acquisition. A publicly visible business contact should not be assumed to authorise indiscriminate electronic marketing. Build referrals, customer-initiated interest and appropriately assessed outreach into the sales process.[^48]

### Payment and finance expansion

South African payment oversight changed recently. SARB's PSMB transition page states that licensing, authorisation and registration functions transferred to SARB from 11 August 2026, with other functions transitioning to PayInc on 2 September. Older generic pages describing PASA processes can therefore mislead if read without the transition update.[^50]

For an initial software product, a qualified payment partner is a more concrete route to explore than assuming the startup can hold money or finance invoices. The precise obligations depend on the activity and arrangement. A payment link, funds custody, marketplace settlement and lending are different products and should be evaluated separately.

### Responsibility remains visible

An evidence record should show who supplied, checked and approved a claim. It should not imply that a software-generated pack certifies legal entitlement, export permission, competence, roadworthiness or clinical safety. The product can reduce administrative failure while preserving the accountable decision-maker.

Before committing to a regulated expansion, obtain advice on the actual proposed flow and country. No regulatory permission, API agreement, partnership, or customer authorisation is established by this report.

## A ninety day validation programme

The purpose of the programme is to select one credible company-building direction while preserving inexpensive options. The dates below are elapsed days from commencement, not commitments or a forecast. Budgets and thresholds are proposed management choices.

| Period | Work | Decision evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1 to 14 | Interview ten qualified buyers in each of two leading segments; examine real cases | Repeated problem, identifiable payer, data access |
| Days 15 to 30 | Reconstruct cases manually and compare incumbents | Measured baseline, specific gap, paid commitments |
| Days 31 to 60 | Run three paid pilots in the stronger segment | Handling-time benefit, quality, usage and onboarding cost |
| Days 61 to 90 | Seek renewals and onboard further similar firms | Retention, repeatable delivery, credible next cohort |

### Interview the work rather than the opinion

Ask the buyer to walk through the last difficult case and the last ordinary case. Identify who touched each, which system held the record, what was missing, how long active work took and what the final result was. Then establish who controls the budget, what alternatives have been tried and what would prevent a switch.

Request a sanitised sample or a controlled review of authorised records. Include both successful and failed cases. Ask the buyer to distinguish dissatisfaction from an actual purchase priority. A signed paid pilot with access to a repeated workflow is much stronger than a letter praising the idea.

### Define the pilot before running it

Specify one workflow, volume range, starting baseline, accountable users, allowable actions, support expectations and the fee. Agree how to measure benefit and what happens when a result is wrong. Measure total human handling time across both the startup and customer, not just the speed of document extraction.

Suggested continuation gates are three paying organisations, sustained use in at least 70% of eligible cases, a 30% reduction in handling time, acceptable reviewed accuracy, and a repeatable onboarding pattern. Renewal after the pilot is necessary evidence. None of these thresholds is an established benchmark or sufficient alone to prove product-market fit.

### Capital allocation

Cap discovery cash spending at a proposed **R20,000 to R40,000**, excluding founder time, before committing to a product build. This is a spending limit to choose, not a supplier-cost estimate. Record specialist work, travel and data preparation separately. A funded team can buy faster learning, but additional engineering should not substitute for customer access.

At day ninety, proceed with one segment, revise one specific assumption, or stop. Pause development if the buyer will not pay, an incumbent resolves the exact problem economically, or delivery remains mostly bespoke. Do not redefine document counts, signups or free pilots as revenue traction because the intended metrics disappoint.

## Portfolio strategy and final recommendation

Wanting several possible ventures is rational. The most useful diversification at this stage is a portfolio of disciplined discoveries, followed by concentrated execution. Several simultaneously operating startups share the same founder attention and financing constraints; several B2B document products also share correlated sales and integration risks.

### Relationship to Skilved

Skilved's current career-record direction makes training evidence a plausible adjacency. It may offer reusable learning about evidence, permissions and institutional workflows, but it is not an independent hedge against the career-platform thesis. Wholesale, contractor and export customers introduce more distinct markets, although their workflow software still shares some technology and execution risk.

Keep new experiments separate in customer discovery and positioning. Reuse proven infrastructure only when useful; do not force unrelated customers into the Skilved brand or build a generic multi-industry platform before learning a specific job. A shared codebase does not create a shared sales channel.

### Recommended next allocation of effort

First, attempt to obtain three distributor case samples and three subcontractor payment-application samples. This access test is itself informative. Run the two discovery tracks with equal discipline, then select the one showing clearer willingness to pay and a gap that survives incumbent comparison.

Second, maintain export evidence as a conditional option while looking for a credible practitioner and anchor customer. Avoid commissioning a build until they provide repeated cases and clarify the downstream acceptance process. If contractor relationships reveal procurement as the more urgent problem, substitute that experiment rather than accumulating another product.

Third, revisit care, waste, specialist resale and parent activities only when a domain partner or distribution asset changes the entry economics. They offer different customers and potential growth paths, but none becomes attractive merely because its current coordination happens in WhatsApp.

### Evidence limits that can change the ranking

Competitor pages establish advertised features and prices, not product quality, local availability, customer retention or implementation outcomes. Vendor case studies are selected examples. Official statistics describe their stated population and period, not the precise customer niches proposed here. The exact reachable customer counts, integration permissions, purchase budgets and repeatable acquisition costs remain unmeasured.

The decisive research now is commercial: actual workflows, paid pilots, competitor comparisons and renewals. The strongest business may emerge from a narrower problem than any headline in this report, or the evidence may reject the leading candidates entirely. Both are valuable outcomes if discovered before a large build.

**Recommendation:** begin with wholesale claims and contractor payment evidence as competing discovery options. Build only the winner. Seek to become the reliable record and execution layer for a valuable transaction, then earn expansion into adjacent work. That is the version of vertical unbundling most consistent with South Africa first execution and global venture ambition.
