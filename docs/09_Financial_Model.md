## Financial Philosophy

Skilved's financial model has three defining characteristics:

1. **Zero marginal cost scaling on the worker side.** Adding the 10,000th worker costs the same as adding the 10th — the agents do the work.

2. **Revenue compounds with graph quality.** As the outcome graph grows, match quality improves. As match quality improves, employer willingness to pay increases.

3. **The intelligence layer creates entirely new revenue categories.** The Skills Pulse Dashboard, Career Simulation Engine, and Employer Accountability Layer open government data license revenue, premium listing fees, and faster Premium conversion — none of which existed before v5.0.

## Intelligence Layer Revenue Impact — NEW v2.0

### Why the Intelligence Layer Changes the Financial Model

The four new intelligence capabilities (Skills Pulse, Career Simulation, Employer Accountability, Collective Intelligence) don't just add revenue streams — they change the financial profile of the entire business:

**1. Faster Premium conversion**
Career Simulation shows workers the exact ROI of upgrading to Premium Level 3. "Your agent could have applied to those 12 opportunities automatically." Conversion from simulation → Premium is estimated at 8% vs 3% industry average. This alone adds R12,800/month in Month 3 from simulation-driven conversions.

**2. New government revenue category**
The Skills Pulse Dashboard opens a revenue category that didn't exist: government data licenses. This is high-value (R200K–R1M/year per customer), low-cost to serve (database queries + PDF generation), and strategically irreplaceable (no competitor has this data). First license target: Month 6.

**3. Employer sticky behaviour**
Employer accountability scores create a new reason for employers to stay on Skilved — their score improves as more candidates go through Skilved and report positive outcomes. This increases employer retention and LTV.

**4. Premium listing fees**
Employers with good scores pay to feature them. This is effectively monetising the quality the platform has already measured — pure margin.

**5. Career Simulation API (Phase 3)**
White-labelling the Career Simulation Engine for TVET colleges and SETAs creates a B2B SaaS category that generates recurring revenue from the institutions that are already Skilved's partners.
