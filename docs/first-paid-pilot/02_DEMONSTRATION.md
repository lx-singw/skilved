# Skilved demonstration: from scattered records to clear next actions

**Fictional demonstration. All people, records, actions and results below are invented examples. No real documents were processed, messages sent, confirmations obtained or outcomes achieved. These tables show the proposed service output; they are not screenshots of functioning Skilved software.**

## Scenario

Example Training Programme wants to prepare five learners for a receiving organisation. Its coordinator chooses these five checks for this example:

1. CV available.
2. Requested qualification evidence available.
3. Permission for the intended sharing recorded.
4. Supervisor confirmation received.
5. Destination-specific form complete.

These are illustrative checks, not universal programme requirements. A real customer chooses its own checklist.

## Before: the coordinator's starting register

P = present in this fictional register. M = missing. P does not mean officially verified.

| Learner | CV | Qualification evidence | Sharing permission | Supervisor confirmation | Destination form | Present |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| L001 | P | P | P | M | M | 3/5 |
| L002 | P | M | P | P | P | 4/5 |
| L003 | P | P | P | M | P | 4/5 |
| L004 | P | P | M | P | P | 4/5 |
| L005 | P | P | P | P | M | 4/5 |

**Baseline: 25 checks; 19 items present; 6 missing; 0 of 5 records has all five items present.**

## The working output: an action queue

| Item | Action | Owner | Illustrative target | What closes the item |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L001 supervisor | Coordinator requests confirmation against the relevant activity. | Coordinator / supervisor | Day 3 | Supervisor response recorded and reviewed by coordinator. |
| L001 form | Identify unanswered fields and prepare a draft using existing facts. | Linda / coordinator / learner | Day 3 | Learner supplies missing facts; coordinator reviews completed form. |
| L002 qualification | Ask whether the document is available in the provider's existing system. | Coordinator | Day 3 | Coordinator identifies the appropriate file; authenticity remains separately recorded. |
| L003 supervisor | Request confirmation; keep pending if unavailable. | Coordinator / supervisor | Day 4 | Attributed response received; otherwise explicit pending status. |
| L004 permission | Obtain the appropriate permission through the provider's existing process. | Coordinator / learner | Day 3 | Permission for this recipient and purpose is recorded. No sharing before this. |
| L005 form | Request the missing enrolment detail. | Coordinator / learner | Day 4 | Actual required detail supplied; no guessed value. |

## Example message draft for the coordinator

> Hello [learner]. We are preparing your records for [specific purpose]. We still need [specific item]. Please use our existing [secure channel] by [agreed date], or let us know if you cannot obtain it. Please do not send documents to a group chat. Contact [coordinator] if anything is unclear.

A second consolidated follow-up covers only items still unresolved. The example does not assume Linda has permission to message learners directly.

## After: an illustrative final register

Suppose four missing items are resolved. L003's supervisor response and L005's form detail remain outstanding.

| Learner | Items present | Final status | Remaining action |
|---|---:|---|---|
| L001 | 5/5 | Ready for provider review | Provider reviews and decides whether to submit. |
| L002 | 5/5 | Ready for provider review | Provider reviews and decides whether to submit. |
| L003 | 4/5 | Waiting for supervisor | Coordinator follows up on the agreed next date. |
| L004 | 5/5 | Ready for provider review | Provider reviews and decides whether to submit. |
| L005 | 4/5 | Waiting for learner | Coordinator requests the remaining form detail. |

**Illustrative result: 23/25 items present; 4 of the original 6 missing items resolved; 2 outstanding; 3 records ready for provider review.**

No qualification verification, recipient acceptance, job placement, measured time saving or actual customer result is implied.

## Five-minute demonstration script

**0:00–0:45 — Establish the problem.**
“This is a fictional example. Imagine these five learners are in your current cohort. The documents may exist across email, spreadsheets and your existing system. The first job is to agree what each record actually needs.”

**0:45–1:45 — Show the baseline.**
“There are six missing items. The register makes those gaps visible without treating a missing document as a judgement about the learner.”

**1:45–3:00 — Show the action queue.**
“Each gap has an owner and a next action. I prepare the consolidated follow-up; your coordinator uses your existing channels. We use the systems you already have.”

**3:00–4:00 — Show the final register.**
“In this illustrative outcome, four gaps are resolved and two remain. You receive both the updated records and a clear handover for the outstanding work. Your team retains the review and submission decisions.”

**4:00–5:00 — Connect to their situation and make the offer.**
“Which of these steps currently takes your team the most time? If this fits a real current cohort, I can handle ten records against five agreed checks for R1,500 over seven working days. Would you be willing to start a paid pilot once we agree the scope?”

Do not describe this as a live product demo or imply the example's improvement is guaranteed.
