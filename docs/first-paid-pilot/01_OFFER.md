# Skilved: learner-record completion pilot

Prepared 6 September 2026. Proposed first-customer offer; editable before sending.

**Get a clear view of incomplete learner records and the next action for each one.**

For a training provider or youth programme with an existing cohort, existing records and one coordinator responsible for them.

## The offer

For **R1,500 total**, I will review up to **10 learner records against up to five requirements you specify**, organise missing items, prepare two consolidated follow-up lists for your coordinator, and deliver a final progress report.

Delivery: **seven working days after we agree the checklist and you provide the authorised records and nominated coordinator**. We agree the actual start date before payment. This is a hands-on pilot delivered by Linda using Skilved's developing workflow and ordinary document tools.

## What you receive

1. A baseline register showing which required items are present, missing or need your review.
2. A named next action and responsible person for every unresolved item.
3. Two consolidated follow-up lists and message drafts for your coordinator to send through your existing channels.
4. An updated register, a provider-reviewed completion checklist for each learner, and a final summary of resolved and outstanding items.
5. A 20-minute handover and one round of corrections to the delivered register/report.

Files remain in an agreed customer-controlled location. Each requirement is marked separately. A document being present does not mean its authenticity or a learner's competence has been verified.

## Your part

- Nominate one coordinator and confirm your authority to share the information for this purpose.
- Agree up to five requirements before the work begins.
- Provide only the minimum relevant records, preferably a redacted status register and references to files in your own system.
- Have your coordinator contact learners and supervisors and confirm any assessments or decisions.
- Review the draft handover within two working days of receiving it.

## Boundaries

The fee covers one cohort, ten records, five agreed checks per record, two follow-up rounds and one correction round. Additional records or work require a separate agreed quote.

The pilot does not include finding placements, guaranteeing responses, certifying qualifications, formal assessment, funder acceptance, recruitment decisions, system integrations or ongoing support. Missing evidence may remain outstanding; it will have a clear status and next action in the final report.

## Payment and acceptance

- R750 deposit once scope and start date are confirmed.
- R750 balance after the final deliverables meet the agreed checklist below.
- Acceptance means the agreed register, follow-up lists and final report have been delivered accurately, with every unresolved item recorded. It does not depend on every learner or supervisor responding.
- If a deliverable is incomplete or inaccurate, identify it during the review window and I will correct it before requesting the balance. Any cancellation and treatment of work already performed will be agreed in writing before work starts.

**Reply to proceed:**

> Please send the pilot scope and payment request for [organisation], covering [number, maximum 10] records. Our coordinator is [name]. Our preferred start date is [date].

Linda will confirm capacity, supplier/payment details and the written scope before payment. No subscription or renewal is automatic.

## How we judge the pilot

We compare missing/review items at the start and end, record coordinator time and document what still needs attention. Time savings are measured, not promised. The next cohort receives its own quote based on what we learn.
