# Sales, demonstration and customer delivery

Date: 11 September 2026. Status: proposed sales process and unvalidated offer. No outreach is sent or customer payment established by this document.

## First buyer hypothesis

Priority update, 12 September 2026: qualify TVET placement/WIL offices, lead employers/programme operators, SDF practices and skills development providers using the [institutional buyer model](03_TVET_SDF_AND_EMPLOYER_BUSINESS_MODEL.md). Identify the actual paying entity and approver. The consumer tier is entirely free. The small manual pilot is optional evidence work towards repeatable software, not the permanent revenue strategy or an applicant-paid service.

A programme coordinator, training provider or small youth-support organisation may pay to reduce missing-record follow-up and prepare usable learner handovers. The buyer must have a current operational problem, authority to provide relevant records, a budget owner and a recipient who will use the output. A learner expressing interest is valuable research but does not validate this buyer hypothesis.

## Concrete offer

Use the existing [pilot offer](../../first-paid-pilot/01_OFFER.md): proposed R1,500 for up to ten learner records, five agreed requirements and two prepared follow-ups, delivered in seven working days after complete agreed intake, with an eight-hour internal effort assumption and proposed R750 deposit/balance. Confirm the exact accepted scope and terms in a written order before work. No placement, bursary award or official qualification outcome is guaranteed.

The public Skilved MVP proceeds as a product track. This manual service tests willingness to pay and workflow value; it is not proof of recurring SaaS demand or a requirement that users first recruit an employer.

## Discovery conversation

Ask for the last real intake or reporting deadline: what arrived, what was missing, how many follow-ups occurred, who checked completeness, what system was used and what consequence followed. Ask them to show a redacted artefact if appropriate. Establish current effort/cost, who approves spending and whether the output would be accepted. Avoid asking only whether they “like the idea”.

Qualify out requests for guaranteed placements, broad access to unrelated private records, uncontrolled scraping or custom integrations outside the offer. Offer a smaller lawful scope where useful; record the reason a prospect is unsuitable without treating it as product failure.

## Demonstration script

1. Explain that the example is fictional and does not show real learners or verified credentials.
2. Show a coordinator's messy intake transformed into a requirements view with explicit unknowns.
3. Open one learner's selected record and show the origin/scope of evidence.
4. Prepare one bounded follow-up draft and show that sending requires the appropriate authority/channel.
5. Produce a recipient-readable handover pack and demonstrate that unrelated private data is excluded.
6. Explain scope, price, delivery assumptions, acceptance and next step.

Use [demonstration materials](../../first-paid-pilot/02_DEMONSTRATION.md). A demo should take approximately ten minutes initially, but its success is customer understanding and a concrete decision, not a rigid presentation duration.

## Commercial stages

| Stage | Evidence to advance |
|---|---|
| Prospect | Relevant organisation and reason for fit |
| Problem confirmed | Recent concrete workflow and consequence |
| Qualified | Authority, budget path, safe data scope and recipient |
| Offer presented | Exact deliverables, price, terms and decision date |
| Committed | Accepted agreement and required payment/authorisation |
| Delivered | Accepted output and measured effort/cost |
| Repeat opportunity | Another real need or renewal decision |

Keep private contact details and conversation notes outside the public repository. Track lost reasons honestly: no urgency, no budget, wrong buyer, existing tool adequate, trust, scope or timing.

## Delivery procedure

Follow [delivery playbook](../../first-paid-pilot/03_DELIVERY_PLAYBOOK.md): validate intake authority, define fields/requirements, obtain minimal data through a safe channel, agree acceptance, process records with attributable status, review exceptions, produce selected outputs, obtain acceptance and hand over. Track time by intake, cleaning, communication, preparation, review and handover. Do not hide unpaid rework in the product-development budget.

Change requests receive an impact on deliverable, price and schedule before work proceeds. A request outside ten records/five requirements is not silently included because the buyer is enthusiastic. Confirm refund/cancellation and dispute handling under the actual agreement and applicable law.

## Success and next evidence

Record payment received, actual delivery hours/cost, missing-record reduction, recipient acceptance, buyer satisfaction in their words, defects and repeat request. A testimonial requires explicit permission and accurate wording. The strongest next commercial evidence is a buyer who pays for the scoped result and then wants another useful outcome.
