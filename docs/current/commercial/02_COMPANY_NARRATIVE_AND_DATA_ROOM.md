## Short narrative

Monetisation decision, 12 September 2026: consumers pay nothing. Institutions are proposed buyers of defined programme-evidence, coordination and reporting workflows. The [TVET/SDF/employer strategy](03_TVET_SDF_AND_EMPLOYER_BUSINESS_MODEL.md) distinguishes evidence of a reporting need from proof of budget, procurement approval or willingness to buy.

Skilved helps people turn learning and work into attributable evidence they can reuse for opportunities. It connects opportunity discovery, preparation, selected sharing and continuing progress, with background tasks that follow explicit user mandates. The initial reachable community is apprentice peers and school leavers in South Africa. The public MVP includes bursaries, learnerships, apprenticeships, internships, graduate programmes and jobs.

Its proposed advantage is completing a useful journey across fragmented tools and institutions while preserving user control. Matching, profiles, AI drafts, job tracking, simulations and coordinator tools already exist elsewhere. Differentiation must therefore be demonstrated through better task outcomes, trusted evidence handling, recipient acceptance and affordable operations.

## Evidence ledger for public claims

Every numerical or affiliation claim has a source, date, owner and permitted wording. A benchmark claim names comparator/version, task, sample, method and limitations. A partner logo requires appropriate permission. Revenue must specify period, currency, cash versus recognised basis and refunds. Outcomes distinguish self-report from corroboration and avoid attributing all results to Skilved.

Do not publish “best in every benchmark”, “government integrated”, “officially verified”, “zero-rated”, “certified compliant” or “guaranteed placement” without exact supporting evidence and scope. Ambition belongs in the vision; achieved results belong in the evidence ledger.

## Restricted diligence room structure

- Corporate: actual formation/ownership records, material decisions and IP agreements.
- Commercial: executed customer/vendor contracts, pipeline methodology, accepted case studies.
- Finance: reconciled accounts, current budget assumptions, commitments and tax/accounting records.
- Product/research: consent-safe findings, cohort definitions, roadmap and benchmark methods.
- Engineering: architecture, capability inventory, deployments and dependency licences.
- Security/privacy: control evidence, data inventory, reviewed policies, incident summaries and relevant assessments.
- People: accepted role agreements, confidentiality/IP and access responsibilities.

Use access-controlled sharing with a document owner, version, sensitivity and expiry. The public Git repository contains templates and sanitised plans, not raw corporate/customer records. Providing a room structure does not mean these records exist.

## Milestone-based funding case

The initial funding case should explain how a defined amount buys a specific validated milestone: reliable public journey, measured repeated use, paid delivery or accepted institutional workflow. Include downside, time uncertainty, operating cost after credits and founder capacity. Broader expansion is conditional on evidence and local readiness. Personal wealth is a possible eventual consequence of a successful sustainable business, not a product milestone or promised return.
