# Roles, hiring and working practices

Date: 11 September 2026. Status: future operating design. Skilved does not have a staffed corporate team merely because role-specific documents exist.

## Initial founder role

The founder combines product, engineering, research, operations and commercial learning around an apprenticeship. Plan against observed sustainable hours, protect essential obligations and keep parallel commitments visible. AI assistance can accelerate analysis and implementation, but it does not remove testing, customer access, judgement or accountability.

## Role sequence driven by evidence

| Need | Candidate contribution | Trigger and acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Source quality overload | Part-time reviewer/operator | Measured backlog/effort exceeds capacity; sample review quality and confidentiality training |
| User support/research coverage | Support/research collaborator | Users wait beyond promised coverage; accurate triage and consent-safe notes |
| Sensitive implementation assurance | Experienced security/engineering reviewer | Private-data/multi-tenant/institutional risk needs independent review; concrete findings and retest |
| Repeated buyer demand | Customer delivery/commercial support | Qualified paid demand exceeds founder delivery capacity; accepted scoped outputs and sound records |
| Proven product scale | Dedicated engineering/design roles | Funded sustained workload and clear ownership; observable delivery outcomes |

Do not hire against imagined growth or carry the historical full-time payroll into the initial budget. Define affordable scope, working arrangement and outcome first.

## Selection and engagement

Use a relevant bounded work discussion or fairly scoped assessment with transparent expectations. Avoid extracting substantial unpaid production work from applicants. Review actual competence, communication, reliability and care for user data. Do not infer suitability from educational prestige alone; the product's evidence philosophy should apply internally too.

Establish the appropriate agreement, payment, confidentiality, intellectual-property/licence rights, work scope and legal classification with qualified advice where needed. A friend, family member or investor does not automatically become an employee, administrator or product decision maker.

## Onboarding

Provide product purpose, current capability status, role scope, applicable policies, least-privilege access, synthetic test data, source/evidence labels, support escalation and one supervised task. Confirm understanding of private records, minors, untrusted source content and unauthorised external actions. Record access owner and review date.

## Working practices

Use a small visible queue and complete useful slices. Record decisions where future collaborators can find them. Raise errors early without punishing honest reporting. Review actual outcomes and process failures rather than hours online or message volume. Preserve focused time and realistic support coverage; do not promise 24/7 work because agents run in the background.

A two-week review covers delivered evidence, blocked work, user learning, capacity and next priorities. Monthly review covers cash, commitments, risk and role load. AI-generated code/content receives the same accountability and validation as other work; generated text is not an independent expert sign-off.

## Offboarding

Revoke repository/cloud/vendor access, sessions, tokens, support rights and organisation roles; recover assets and transfer task ownership; rotate shared credentials that should not have been shared; retain only justified records. Confirm IP/deliverable handover under the actual agreement. Do not erase audit or employment/contract records indiscriminately.

## Career and sustainability

Use Skilved development to build the founder's own attributable portfolio: shipped features, research methods, test evidence, operational fixes and paid outcomes, excluding confidential information. The apprenticeship remains valuable experience and income. A strong project career develops through useful delivery and trusted relationships; neither dropping out nor working excessive hours guarantees a successful company.
