# Coverage of the pre-build and future-direction discussion

Date: 24 September 2026. Status: documentation reconciliation of the visible founder conversation. Owner: founder/product with assistant-supported documentation. This register records substantive ideas, qualifications, proposed actions and reported findings; it does not convert suggestions into accepted product commitments or establish their implementation.

## Scope and method

Subsequent 24 September decision: after a separate review of external feedback, the founder authorised the [operational refinements OP01–OP08](08_OPERATIONAL_REFINEMENTS_2026-09-24.md), preserving the ambition and scope. That register extends this historical FC01–FC56 coverage and records the accepted subset; it does not convert every earlier recommendation or external claim into an accepted requirement.

The coverage boundary is the user-facing discussion beginning with “What should I do next, before we start building?”, followed by the request to anticipate near and distant developments, the request for thorough help building a consequential company with AI, and the request to document that discussion exhaustively. Earlier repository strategy is referenced where relevant. This is not a verbatim transcript or a claim to have investigated every possible future business, technical or legal question.

Method: compare those user requests and substantive assistant responses with the future brief, baseline audit and current M0 documents; preserve missing detail; record evolved suggestions; map each point to an explicit home; verify local link targets. Repeated rhetoric is consolidated. Internal research leads and unrelated alternative-company research are not silently adopted as Skilved commitments.

## Reconciliations and corrections

1. The initial answer proposed 3–5 participants; later planning suggested 5–8. Both are retained with their relationship explained. Neither establishes statistical power, a fixed launch gate or an obligation to conduct two rounds.
2. Six starter links and 2–3 examples per category are successive preparation suggestions, not contradictory launch inventory requirements. Actual availability and source review control publication.
3. Progress after opportunity closure was explicit in the first brief; the later conversation also specified rejection. Both are now explicit, with evidence validity and recipient acceptance preserved.
4. The mission wording evolved. Both formulations are retained as proposals; no final tagline was silently selected.
5. Voice and language were discussed as later access options. They are now explicitly documented without being added to M0.
6. The initial user-facing delivery-plan link used an invalid placeholder URL. The real local home is [the M0 delivery plan](../planning/03_M0_COMPANION_DELIVERY_PLAN.md). The register preserves the correct destination, not the broken link.
7. A technical baseline audit was performed, but only bounded commands and selected static inspection. A full route/security audit, successful build, user trial and deployment are still outstanding.
8. A current-document index header dated 11 September describes the original baseline. Dated 13 and 24 September updates below it record later additions; the new strategy is a recommendation with its own date, not a rewrite of historical acceptance.
