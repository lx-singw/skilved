## 3. What is explicitly not adopted

- A trade-only launch, exclusive artisan curation or a fixed number of listings as automatic proof of authority/usefulness.
- Wholesale agent/shared-package removal as a prescribed prerequisite, a fixed small error count, or a promised one-day passing build. Dependency and route inspection determines the implementation.
- A permanent, data-free WhatsApp backup or automatic preservation of personal progress. Ordinary public sharing and private-state disclosure remain separate.
- Generic checklist facts such as real-looking employer deadlines, certificate ages or qualification requirements without the actual issuer source. The canonical Skilved page is not labelled as the original application destination.
- Automatic net-stipend filters, guessed taxi fares, relocation assumptions or claims that a commute guarantees dropout.
- Invented deadline urgency, assumed application fees or mandatory contact routes that the issuer has not supplied.
- Archiving institutional strategy, requiring active-user traction as proof of buyer demand, or assuming uniform public/private procurement times and purchasing authority.
- Blanket claims about applicant unwillingness to share, inevitable failure from using AI, every college's legal classification, a verified user-count interpretation, deployed hosting location, founder income or available hours. Such claims are not a basis for these decisions.

The relevant useful questions remain testable: whether people share voluntarily; which sources produce relevant results; whether exported public checklists help; what connectivity and travel barriers occur; which learning support improves independent capability; and which organisations can buy a valuable workflow. Proposed measurements do not establish their answers.
