## 3. Claims and examples that must not become product facts

| Proposal claim | Disposition | Replacement / evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp literally stripped Facebook messaging into a startup | Historical analogy only | WhatsApp developed independently and joined Facebook in 2014; focus is a design lesson, not a proven success formula |
| LinkedIn/Indeed are unable to understand structured local requirements | Rejected absolute claim | LinkedIn publishes structured job fields; specialisation may improve our experience but is copyable |
| A competitor cannot imitate without breaking its economics | Unvalidated counter-positioning hypothesis | Requires evidence of a business-model conflict; adding a checklist or field does not establish one |
| Guaranteed defeat of competitors, unassailable wedge or inevitable failure of every broad board | Rejected prediction | Measure scoped user preference, task performance, cost and demand |
| Top 10%/remaining 90% audience split; 60% scam posts; fixed 50-person moderation need | Unsupported supplied statistics | Do not use in pitch, copy, risk sizing or staffing estimates without suitable evidence |
| Indeed principally earns from Google AdSense clutter | Unsupported/misleading description | Its own material describes employer-sponsored job visibility; avoid invented competitor economics |
| Specific competitor pages consume 15MB/R5; our pages are instantly below 100KB | Unsupported measurement/promise | Measure scoped devices, networks, pages and payloads before publishing performance claims |
| Handshake/Otta beat LinkedIn or gained massive share | Unverified comparative claim | Cases can inspire questions; adoption/share/success needs a dated, scoped primary source |
| Every Skilved listing is safe; Scam-Shield Guarantee | Rejected guarantee | Scope-specific check records, uncertainty, monitoring and correction |
| Any fee in every work/study situation proves a scam | Overbroad legal/safety statement | Skilved is free; contextual payment concerns reviewed, statutory wording accurate |
| Any external ATS, social-only issuer or free-mail contact is fake | Rejected blanket rule | Establish issuer/destination relationship and appropriate independent corroboration |
| Illustrative employer salaries, marks, certification ages and dates are live facts | Synthetic examples only | Actual published cards require source evidence and review; never invent qualification alternatives |
| Two reports must arrive before action, or two reports prove fraud | Rejected mechanical rule | A credible serious report can trigger triage; evidence and abuse review guide decisions |
| A social network must never be built; all jobseekers reject social connection | Rejected permanent restriction | Retain staged network; support both solo use and useful relationships |

Do not repeat the detailed unsourced allegations as marketing material. Preserve only the bounded correction record needed for future reviewers. No claim of zero-rated access, carrier partnership, employer accreditation or platform-wide independent security verification follows from this planning work.

## 4. Primary evidence supporting corrections

Sources checked during the 13 September discussion; descriptions support only the stated scope. They do not constitute an independent evaluation of the products.

- [WhatsApp about](https://www.whatsapp.com/about): independent founders and joining Facebook in 2014.
- [LinkedIn job-posting schema](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/talent/job-postings/api/job-posting-api-schema?view=li-lts-2026-03): structured experience, employment and compensation concepts; no proof of local task quality.
- [Indeed pricing](https://www.indeed.com/hire/resources/howtohub/how-pricing-works-on-indeed?hl=en): employer-sponsored visibility; no Skilved pricing benchmark inferred.
- [SA Youth about](https://sayouth.mobi/About/EN) and [Careers Portal](https://www.careersportal.co.za/welcome-to-the-careers-portal): existing local work/learning coverage; locality/categories alone are not unique.
- [Indeed reporting](https://support.indeed.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028156452-Reporting-a-job): competitor reporting capability exists; no response-time comparison established.
- [Employment Services Act, section 15](https://www.labour.gov.za/DocumentCenter/Acts/Public%20Employment%20Services/Employment%20Services%20Act%202014.pdf): employment-service fee prohibition and specified-exception mechanism. Applicability to an actual service and current notices needs appropriate review; this is not a general waiver to charge applicants.
