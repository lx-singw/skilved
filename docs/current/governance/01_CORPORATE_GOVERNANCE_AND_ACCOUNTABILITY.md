# Corporate governance and accountability

Date: 11 September 2026. Status: operating blueprint; incorporation, appointments, tax status, bank accounts and signed agreements are not established by this document.

## Operating principle

Skilved needs clear ownership and reliable records before it needs corporate ceremony. A founder can hold multiple roles, but must name the responsible role, recognise conflicts and avoid claiming independent review when reviewing their own work. This blueprint scales with actual customers, staff and obligations.

## Accountability matrix

| Decision | Accountable role | Required input/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Product scope and release | Founder/product | User evidence, release gates, operational capacity |
| Engineering/security | Engineering owner | Tests, access model, incident readiness |
| Personal-data purposes and notices | Privacy accountable role | Data inventory, applicable obligations, specialist review when needed |
| Public claims and partnerships | Founder/commercial | Source, agreement and exact permission to claim affiliation |
| Spending/contracts | Founder/authorised signatory | Available funds, term, recurring liability, exit path |
| Financial records and filings | Finance role with qualified adviser as needed | Actual entity/tax facts, invoices and reconciliations |
| Safeguarding | Named safeguarding lead before affected feature | Escalation procedure, coverage and suitable review |
| Major incident | Incident commander | Incident record, containment and notification assessment |

Roles are unfilled except where the founder explicitly accepts them. A diagram is not a legal appointment or a service contract.

## Formation and statutory readiness register

Before trading through a company, establish the actual entity and contracting party, registration details, ownership/securities records, bank/signing authority, tax/accounting treatment, applicable filings, information officer responsibilities and contracts. Do not put an invented registration number, VAT number or director title on an invoice.

CIPC connects annual-return filing with beneficial-ownership information and related records; exact obligations must be checked against the actual entity and current rules. See [CIPC annual-return and beneficial-ownership guidance](https://www.cipc.co.za/?p=20728). Maintain a private filing calendar with trigger date, responsible person, evidence and next review, rather than copying an unverified universal deadline into code.

Use a qualified South African adviser for incorporation, employment/contractor arrangements, equity, tax and consequential legal documents when applicable. This planning package is not executed legal advice or a substitute for filings.

## Funding and ownership discipline

Classify each incoming contribution as customer revenue, gift, loan, equity or another documented instrument. Record amount, rights, repayment or dilution terms, conditions and signatures as appropriate. Personal closeness does not determine shareholder rights. Do not exchange equity informally to solve a small temporary hosting expense without understanding the permanent consequence.

Keep founder finances and company/project accounts distinguishable. The founder's apprenticeship, household obligations and available time are planning inputs, not company assets. No debt, spending, investment solicitation or equity issue is authorised by the documentation.

## Decision and meeting records

Use a short monthly operating review: cash and commitments; user outcomes and unresolved harm; delivery versus estimate; source/service reliability; customer commitments; security/privacy risks; upcoming decisions. Record decisions with date, owner, evidence, options, consequence and revisit trigger. For legally significant company decisions, use the appropriate formal process and records for the actual entity.

Document conflicts of interest, related-party transactions and ownership of contributed intellectual property. Staff/contractors retain only access needed for their role; departures trigger access and asset review. Maintain confidentiality without suppressing legitimate safety reporting.

## Public versus private records

The repository may contain product strategy, sanitised decisions and templates. It must not contain private shareholder details, identity documents, banking information, passwords, customer rosters, confidential agreements or personal relationship history. Establish a restricted corporate record location before collecting those materials.

## Stage gates

R0 records ownership and operating assumptions. R2 establishes a real contracting identity for any paid pilot, appropriate notices, support contact and cash controls. R3 adds customer/vendor agreements and tenant accountability. R4/R5 add assurance, staffing and country-specific obligations only as the business actually expands. [Risk management](02_RISK_AND_DEPENDENCY_REGISTER.md) and [financial controls](../finance/01_FINANCIAL_MODEL_AND_CONTROLS.md) provide the supporting routines.
