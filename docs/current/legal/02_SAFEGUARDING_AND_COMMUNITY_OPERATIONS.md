# Safeguarding and community operations

Date: 11 September 2026. Status: proposed safety design. Under-18 private/social participation requires an appropriate reviewed process and actual operating coverage before enabling those features.

## Scope and principles

Skilved's ambition includes high-school and post-matric users. Public discovery can deliver value before private youth networking exists. Design age-appropriate participation deliberately; do not introduce direct contact, public rankings or open recruitment messages simply to increase virality.

Restrict unnecessary exposure of identity, location, school schedule, contact details and private evidence. Use specific invited requests and bounded groups. A school or coordinator relationship is not automatic permission to see every learner record or act as a guardian for every purpose.

## Risk controls

| Risk | Required product/operating response |
|---|---|
| Unwanted adult contact | Appropriate invitation/contact boundaries, block/report and moderation escalation |
| Public shaming or ranking | No public “least employable” or completion league tables; private constructive feedback |
| Scam recruitment or fee demands | Source review, clearly visible report route, investigation and affected-user correction |
| Coercive evidence sharing | Granular selection, understandable visibility and revocation; alternatives where possible |
| Sensitive school/workplace material | Upload guidance, restricted access, report/removal process |
| Harassment through help/referee requests | Rate controls, participant choice, decline/block, audit and escalation |
| Misleading credentials | Specific claim labels, correction/dispute workflow and no invented authority |

## Report handling

Receive the report without requiring the reporter to repeat harmful details publicly. Capture minimal evidence in a restricted record. Assess urgency, affected users and immediate containment; block or limit the relevant interaction when justified. Preserve evidence appropriately and route to the named safeguarding/privacy/security role. Determine any external reporting duties with suitable current guidance for the circumstances; do not promise confidentiality beyond what can lawfully and safely be provided.

Tell the reporter what next step can be shared without exposing another person's private information. Provide a fair review/appeal route for moderation decisions. Record reason, evidence, action, owner and review. A model can assist triage but cannot be the sole unaccountable decision maker for consequential safety actions.

## Participation readiness

Before affected youth features, establish actual age scope, lawful processing approach, guardian/competent-person handling where required, school/organisation responsibilities, public/private visibility defaults, staff training, reporting contact and coverage, escalation path, data minimisation and incident tabletop. Apply relevant POPIA and other obligations using [trust/privacy analysis](../security/10_TRUST_PRIVACY_AND_SAFETY.md) and qualified review where needed.

If readiness is incomplete, keep the affected private/social capability unavailable and offer safe public information or an appropriate supported alternative. This is a feature-specific gate, not removal of the long-term high-school audience.

## Community health metrics

Measure resolved useful requests, unwanted-contact reports, time to protective action within actual coverage, repeat abuse, appeal outcomes and participant understanding of visibility. Do not publish identifying small-cohort breakdowns. Growth is acceptable only when helpful interactions and operating capacity support it.
