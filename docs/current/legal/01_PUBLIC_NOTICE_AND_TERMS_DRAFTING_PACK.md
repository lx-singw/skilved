## Privacy notice outline

1. Who operates Skilved and how to contact the accountable privacy role.
2. What each service collects and why, including optional versus necessary fields.
3. How public opportunities, private career records, evidence, agent tasks and organisation workspaces differ.
4. What selected sharing exposes and how expiry/revocation works, including downloaded-copy limits.
5. When providers or authorised organisations receive data and under what safeguards.
6. How AI assists, what is sent to providers, the scope of human/user review and correction.
7. Retention, deletion, backups and justified exceptions using the actual adopted schedule.
8. Access/correction/objection/deletion routes and identity verification proportional to the request.
9. Children/age-appropriate participation and the actual guardian/school process where applicable.
10. Complaints, regulator route, version date and material-change communication.

The wording must reflect current law and actual operations. Use the [trust policy](../security/10_TRUST_PRIVACY_AND_SAFETY.md) and [data governance](../governance/03_DATA_GOVERNANCE_AND_RETENTION.md) as inputs, then reconcile every statement with implementation.

## Service terms drafting decisions

Define the service as assistance and record/preparation functionality within the implemented scope. Explain source uncertainty and evidence provenance without disclaiming responsibility for Skilved's own conduct. Establish the user's ownership/licence relationship for submitted material and Skilved's limited processing rights. Avoid perpetual unrestricted rights to private learner evidence for unrelated AI training.

Specify conduct restrictions, fair suspension/report/appeal handling, account closure/export, material service changes and customer support. Distinguish paid organisation services from free learner functions. Assess consumer, employment-service and other applicable obligations for the actual offering before adopting liability, charging or dispute clauses. Do not invent waiver language that purports to remove non-waivable rights.

## Interface notices at the point of action

Upload: explain purpose, audience, file handling and unnecessary sensitive material. Share: show exact items, recipient mode, expiry and download consequence. Activate agent: show action, resources, trigger, duration and pause/cancel. Invite referee: show requested claim and access scope. Join organisation: show visibility and leaving/revocation effects. Public contribution: warn clearly about intended public audience and reporting.

A long privacy page does not replace understandable notices at these consequential moments. Do not bundle unrelated optional marketing or public sharing into necessary account creation.
