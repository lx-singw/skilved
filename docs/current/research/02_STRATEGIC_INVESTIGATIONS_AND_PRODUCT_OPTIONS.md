## 1. The decision this research must inform

Find the smallest repeatable problem that learners want solved, institutions will pay to resolve, recipients accept and Skilved can operate sustainably. Preserve the complete free consumer career product and all six opportunity categories. The institutional revenue hypothesis must earn its place through observed workflow and buying evidence.

The most consequential uncertainty is **whether the proposed institutional workflow is already handled adequately by an existing platform, and where work still fails between people and systems**. Moving from a job board to digital learner records does not automatically create differentiation. Avoid solving the coordinator's visible inconvenience while missing the learner's actual obstacle or the buyer's spending constraints.

## 4. Product directions unlocked by evidence

These are options to investigate, not an instruction to merge all of them into the MVP.

| Candidate direction | Trigger to pursue | Evidence that would weaken it |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation and exception resolution alongside existing systems | Repeated errors occur before upload/submission and current tools do not resolve them efficiently | Existing module solves the task with a small configuration/training change |
| Recipient-compatible evidence handover | Same evidence is repeatedly reconstructed for different legitimate recipients | Recipients cannot or will not accept generated/selected outputs; useful interoperability cannot be obtained |
| Supervisor confirmation of specific work | Credible observations are missing because the approval workflow is cumbersome | Supervisors cannot observe the claimed activity or reminders simply shift burden onto them |
| Programme milestone and renewal assistance | Real deadlines and evidence requirements recur after placement | Another system already handles them adequately or use is too infrequent to justify maintenance |
| Host-readiness coordination with an existing network | Placement failures concern matching real workplace capacity, mentoring and programme requirements | Few legitimate hosts exist and Skilved cannot improve supply/coordination without a large separate operation |
| Permissioned SDF multi-client workspace | An SDF practice has repeated client work, authority and budget | Tenant/rule/support complexity outweighs reusable value or each client requires a wholly bespoke service |
| Personal record continuity after leaving a programme | Learners use selected evidence with a new recipient and retain independent access | Records are unused outside mandatory reporting or recipients do not understand the evidence |
| Authorised credential-verification coordination | Recipients require it, provider access exists and organisation funding covers it | Cost/turnaround/coverage do not fit the user's task, or the proposed flow lacks proper authority |

The strongest current hypothesis is **helping a person and their supporters resolve missing or disputed evidence, then hand it to the actual receiving workflow in an accepted form**. Its potential advantage is reduced rework and reliable continuity across boundaries. Existing products may also do this; the hypothesis must be tested directly.

## 5. An initial investigation sequence

### Pass A — recent failures and installed alternatives

Start with a reachable TVET office, employer/programme operator and SDF practice. Seek both operators and budget owners, and include learners and a supervisor/recipient. An initial planning example is two organisation conversations per segment plus several learner/recipient observations; expand or change recruitment when findings diverge. This is qualitative discovery, not a representative survey or a fixed cap.

Ask to see a recent workflow and a redacted artefact. Classify a small initial set, such as 10–20 records where access permits, including successful cases and failures. Do not ask prospects to expose unrelated personal records. Produce a bottleneck map, installed-system map, actual buying process and current limitations. Access and appointment timing are external dependencies; no new calendar commitment is assumed.

### Pass B — accepted output and burden test

Choose one recurring task with a real recipient. Build a synthetic or authorised redacted example in the required output format. Have the operator, learner where relevant and recipient complete it. Measure total effort across participants, missing/incorrect facts, interpretation, rework and acceptance. Compare against the actual existing method, not an artificially weak spreadsheet example.

Test one reusable evidence handover beyond the originating institution. If it fails, identify whether the issue is missing data, lack of trusted origin, wrong format, recipient policy or no practical need. Each failure implies a different product decision.

### Pass C — paid workflow and repeat use

Present an exact organisational scope and price after understanding cost and buyer authority. Record who can decide, the next decision date, contractual/data requirements and payment terms. Proceed to a bounded agreement only when authorised. Follow one complete cycle and seek another cycle or comparable customer using the same core workflow. No pilot price, commitment or renewal is established by this plan.

Track consumer benefit separately from administrator adoption. A college can mandate attendance in software without demonstrating that a learner values their career copilot. A useful platform needs both institutional value and a learner reason to trust and reuse it.

## 6. Decision rules

- If installed tools already solve the task acceptably, investigate configuration, integration or a different unresolved step before building a replacement.
- If the real recipient rejects the output, fix its acceptance path before optimising the dashboard.
- If users value the workflow but nobody has a budget or workable procurement route, do not label the B2B model validated; test another buyer/channel or pause the paid scope.
- If the product reduces one person's effort by imposing more work or privacy risk on another, redesign and remeasure total burden.
- If each programme requires extensive manual rule interpretation and support, show that cost in pricing/capacity or keep that capability narrowly scoped.
- If a new idea requires sensitive collection, formal assessment, external submissions or cross-client access, its existing feature-specific readiness gates apply before real use.
- If acquisition succeeds but voluntary meaningful return and recipient acceptance do not, investigate value/seasonality rather than adding engagement mechanics automatically.

For quantitative tests, agree thresholds, sampling and comparison before seeing outcomes. Log contradictory findings and failed assumptions. A small convenience sample supports prioritisation; it cannot prove national demand, a success probability or universal superiority.
