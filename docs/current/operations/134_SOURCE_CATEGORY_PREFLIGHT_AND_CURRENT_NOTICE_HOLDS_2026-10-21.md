## Worker category review gates

Before this change, a current deadline and syntactically scoped route could reach AI without any apprenticeship wording, or despite an explicitly conflicting current internship invitation. Preflight now requires the title or body to contain explicit apprenticeship evidence. Missing evidence yields `NOTICE_CATEGORY_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. A narrowly recognised current invitation to join/apply for an internship programme alongside apprenticeship evidence yields `NOTICE_CATEGORY_CONFLICT`.

The same check runs at publication, so an affirmative or cached model result cannot bypass it. Trade-only titles remain eligible when the body supplies apprenticeship evidence. Degrees, diplomas and listing membership do not classify a notice. Past experience, background programmes, negated invitations and recognised historical invitations do not automatically become competing current intakes. Sentence boundaries and explicit current-time wording prevent earlier historical context from hiding a later current invitation.

These are conservative review gates, not a complete natural-language classifier or independent employer verification. The rules do not choose or repair the source's intended category.
