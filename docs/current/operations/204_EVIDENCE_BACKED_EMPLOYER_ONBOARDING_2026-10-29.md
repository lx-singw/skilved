# Evidence-backed employer onboarding and reusable authority

29 October 2026 (SAST). Unknown employer origins now create a bounded, deduplicated onboarding need. A shared collector captures corporate, careers and independent identity evidence; source-role operators can record immutable proposals and make a versioned qualification/rejection decision. A qualified corporate anchor is reused by later notices through the existing [name lookup and corporate backlink](203_NAME_ONLY_CORPORATE_AUTHORITY_2026-10-29.md), without per-advert sources, grants or reviewer login.

This implements an evidence-backed onboarding/review workflow. It does **not** autonomously establish the identity of every unknown company. New authority still needs explicit source-role identity review; an untrusted name, model confidence or two self-claiming websites cannot grant it. Routine ingestion after qualification remains a machine operation. A qualified identity-source automation policy is still needed before unknown-employer qualification itself can run unattended.

## Collection and evidence

`pnpm --filter @skilved/catalogue onboard:employers <private-plan.json>` accepts `{version:1, employers:[...]}` with 1–10 employer plans. Each plan supplies `issuer`, optional reviewed `aliases`, a `corporateUrl` homepage, same-origin `careersUrl`, different-origin `identityUrl`, and optional `needId`. These are candidate data, not company-specific code or granted authority. The identity source's independence/credibility is a review responsibility; different origins alone do not establish it.

The native collector reuses the existing pinned public DNS/TLS client, exact URL allowlists, 500 KB response limit, robots checks, three-second request spacing and normal user agent. It executes no scripts, accepts no cookies, expands no unlisted redirects and performs no AI calls or database writes. Three planned pages and two robots files give at most five bounded retrieval attempts initially. A single uniquely observed, safe same-origin careers candidate can correct a path using at most two additional attempts. Both bundles are retained privately; known identity failures are not retried to force success. The workflow never follows a guessed domain or application URL.

Explicit saved-evidence reuse is limited to matching URLs observed within 24 hours. Original response/error timestamps survive; reuse is not a fresh retrieval. Bundles keep failed/unavailable pages in their three-row denominator. Inert, bounded HTML inspection excludes hidden/executable/form content, refuses base-URL overrides and checks visible identity-to-corporate and corporate-to-careers links. Negative/warning links cannot supply support; a parent careers menu does not inherit a separate child fraud-alert link's warning. Alternate careers paths are suggestions, not authority.

`awaiting_identity_review` means the supported evidence shape is present. `evidence_incomplete` means a page/link/identity/alias check remains unsupported. Neither state qualifies an employer. HTML-only identity evidence is currently supported; inaccessible references, unsupported documents and uncertain identity remain held.

## Private records and qualification

The existing private operator gains `employer-onboarding-record` (`bundleFile`, `reason`) and `employer-onboarding-review` (`review`, `reason`). Both require current source-role authorization. Recording stores separate content-hashed evidence snapshots and an immutable proposal, with no authority write. A review pins `proposalId`, `expectedHash`, `action` and `expectedAuthority`; qualification additionally requires explicit `identityConfirmed`, `identityKind`, an exact identity quote and `expiresAt`.

Qualification re-reads snapshots, verifies the bundle/hash/plan and optional need binding, re-evaluates evidence, rejects future/stale observations, checks the quote/company and limits expiry to 30 days. Existing authority requires its current ID/revision/hash. A duplicate corporate authority under another ID is refused. The derived record has no email authority and is scoped to apprenticeship use, the reviewed corporate host and fixed careers page. Reviewer-supplied arbitrary routes/categories cannot be smuggled into it.

Authority JSON, current indexed relationships, immutable relationship versions, the review decision, linked need state and audit are written atomically. A proposal is reviewed once; changed evidence needs a new proposal. Rejecting a proposal does not revoke an older authority. Renewal requires fresh-enough evidence and current authority preconditions. Sources, machine grants, historical usage and public records are unchanged by onboarding.

`employer_onboarding_needs` keys bind normalized exact issuer name and optional corporate host. They preserve first-seen time and at most 20 recent origin references. Matching unknown-origin events share a need; qualification records `authority_registered`. Revocation/expiry can produce a new unresolved observation. Needs, proposals, snapshots and decisions are private operator records. No public onboarding endpoint, role bootstrap or worker permission to qualify itself is added.

## Mixed-batch and real-source evidence

An authored four-notice batch starts under one publisher/grant with no corporate authority. All four create one shared need and spend no AI calls. One evidence-bound employer review is then completed. In the next normal publisher cycle, with the reviewer account inactive, **two notices reach private drafts and two conflicting originals stay held before AI**. No per-notice source/permission change occurs. The same source-day budget retains five prior mock calls and increases to nine for the four admitted mock extraction/validation calls. These are emulator/model-fixture results, not real-provider accuracy qualification.

Two explicit real employer-root candidates, Sasol and Nampak, were selected from the existing mixed RecentJobs cohort as a small evidence-collection check, not a random or representative sample. Their corporate and careers pages are captured. A generic menu-context fix removes an unrelated child warning, and Nampak's explicitly linked `/about/careers` path is followed with saved evidence retained. The sequence uses **12 bounded native retrieval attempts** in total: ten initial attempts and two for the observed path correction. The independent JSE profile retrievals remain `UNSUPPORTED_RESPONSE`; they are not replaced by publisher claims or browser-derived fake HTML.

Both real proposals therefore remain `evidence_incomplete`. Local source-role qualification attempts cannot override that missing evidence. The original first-ten frozen worker replay still holds every notice before AI and creates one onboarding need, no authority, model calls/budgets, candidates, decisions, drafts or public records. The earlier original-vacancy/date disagreements and email-method gaps remain independent holds. No real employer is qualified in staging.

Real bodies, plans, bundles, comparisons and emulator receipts remain ignored under `.artifacts/groq/employer-onboarding-2026-10-29/` and `.artifacts/shadow/`. No raw source snapshot or provider response is committed. No live cloud, quota, grant, application, publication, scheduler or hosting state changes. Hosting remains undeployed at the founder's request.
