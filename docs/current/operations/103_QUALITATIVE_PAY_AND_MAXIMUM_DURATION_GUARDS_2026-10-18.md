Amount, maximum, currency and period stay unknown in these recognised non-numeric forms. Missing facts, wrong kinds, invented numbers or intervals, and conflicting recognised descriptions cause a hold rather than output repair.

Explicit programme participation/duration sentences and duration labels can identify a maximum of one through ten years, months or weeks. A supported summary or practical passage must retain the upper bound, quantity and unit together. A fixed duration, changed quantity/unit, absent material value or appearance only in a broad location quote does not satisfy this check. Distinct recognised maximums cause a hold. Conditional, negated, alternative, experience-related and unfamiliar wording is left to semantic review; this is not a general duration parser. Recognised publisher preparation sections and promotional lines are excluded from source hints.
