# Service operations and support

13 September M0 support extension: [source/lead/report operations](03_SOURCE_CHECKS_AND_COMMUNITY_CONTRIBUTIONS.md) require a named review owner, realistic coverage, capability-scoped receipts, material-change handling and intake pause when necessary. One credible serious report can trigger review; two reports do not automatically prove fraud. Do not promise 24/7 human support or investigation before a deadline without capacity.

Date: 11 September 2026. Status: proposed operating model, with founder coverage to be confirmed. Read alongside [opportunity operations](09_OPPORTUNITY_OPERATIONS.md).

Do not publish a response-time SLA until actual coverage is known. The initial service is founder-operated around an apprenticeship. Publish support hours and emergency reporting routes that can actually be monitored; automated acknowledgement is not human resolution.

## Operating cadence

**At each operating session:** inspect alerts, unexpected spend, failed/dead-letter tasks, unsafe source reports and pending privacy/security issues. Check that public freshness labels reflect real check status. Handle urgent harm before routine feature work.

**Weekly:** sample active listings across all six categories; examine preparation failures and task cancellations; review user-reported errors; reconcile source permission changes; measure manual minutes per accepted record; inspect outstanding support and upcoming deadlines. Do not assume equal category volume proves equal quality.

**At each delivery review:** review release evidence, incidents, costs versus workload, retention cohort observations and unresolved commitments. Update capacity using actual effort. **Monthly:** reconcile vendor invoices, access roles, retention/deletion jobs, backup evidence, revenue/receivables and major risks.

## Support intake and resolution

Provide one monitored entry point initially. Collect category, affected feature, approximate time, safe reproduction and optional screenshot with redaction guidance. Do not ask for passwords or complete identity documents. Associate a ticket with minimal account metadata only after authentication/verification appropriate to the request.

Classify as product help, data correction, source safety, privacy rights, security incident, billing or accessibility. Assign severity, owner, next action and next contact. Resolve by answering, correcting, repairing or explaining a verified limitation. Record the actual outcome and invite reopening; an automatic reply does not close a case.

## Escalation

Suspected unauthorised access, harmful external actions and safeguarding reports follow the [incident procedure](../security/03_INCIDENT_RESPONSE_AND_RECOVERY.md). A wrong deadline needs a source correction and an assessment of affected saved workspaces. A billing dispute uses the actual agreement and records, not ad hoc promises in chat. A request for formal career, legal or aviation certification goes to the appropriate authority; Skilved can organise evidence without impersonating that authority.

## Quality sampling

Define the sample frame, category, age, source type and critical fields before inspection. Record numerator, denominator and abstentions. Sample both popular and low-volume sources. Use a second reviewer for disputed consequential facts where available, otherwise hold uncertain publication. Separate extraction correctness from whether the source itself is truthful.

## Staffing triggers

Add help when measured review/support load repeatedly exceeds sustainable capacity, urgent incidents cannot be covered, or contractual response needs require another person. Define the work and affordable funding first. Proposed next roles are source/review operations, user support/research, and targeted engineering/security review; no four-person payroll is assumed.

## Operational handover packet

Service owner; environment/resource inventory; monitoring links; safe access process; source register; queue/runbook references; current incidents; pending customer commitments; billing/renewal dates; backup status; known limitations. Store secrets in their designated system, never in this packet. A handover is accepted only after the recipient performs a safe representative operation and knows the escalation path.
