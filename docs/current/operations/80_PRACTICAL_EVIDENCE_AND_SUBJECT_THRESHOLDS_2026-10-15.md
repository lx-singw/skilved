# Practical instruction evidence and standalone subject thresholds

Date: 15 October 2026. Status: all 501 catalogue tests pass with offline regression and rejection guards implemented. No new source/provider requests, quota changes or publication.

## Practical facts retain their full evidence

A stated practicalConditions value must now equal its complete contiguous supporting quote verbatim. Compact extraction and split metadata check this after reconstructing the selected span. Native canonical mapping checks it independently; programme mapping also checks against the original parent notice. Additional prose, paraphrases, shortened conditions and concatenated noncontiguous passages fail AI_PRACTICAL_EVIDENCE_UNRESOLVED or the existing unsupported-evidence check. Code does not replace a bad value with its quote or repair a provider response. Other fields may still use supported paraphrases.

Source-local practicalEvidencePolicy hints identify recognised, optionally numbered Documents to prepare sections. Those sections cannot supply stated practical conditions or stated requirement items, even when copied verbatim. They fail AI_PUBLISHER_PREPARATION_UNRESOLVED. Known subsequent headings, closing labels and promotional boundaries end an exclusion; an unclosed preparation section stays excluded through the end of the source. This is a conservative check for a recognised layout, not general publisher-versus-employer attribution.

Quote-only canonical facts cannot distinguish repeated source occurrences. If any occurrence overlaps preparation context, a short duplicate quote is held; fuller evidence that uniquely belongs to actual required documents can pass. Required application-document sections, complete conditional instructions, form requests, email subjects and original application destinations remain supported. Ambiguous facts and unknown requirement logic retain their existing material holds.

One practical fact still supports one contiguous passage. Required instructions that cannot fit a faithful passage remain for review; the worker does not join separated evidence or silently drop instructions. Verbatim evidence alone also does not establish that every instruction is an issuer obligation. Separate semantic validation and source/issuer qualification remain necessary.

Unknown, unbounded and unfamiliar wording is not silently reclassified.
