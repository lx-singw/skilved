# Shorter title-based location evidence and consistent grouping guidance

Date: 18 October 2026. Status: advisory location hints can recommend a shorter contiguous range using an exact title repetition of a recognised location label. Validation now distinguishes a separate standalone subject exclusion from a qualification's attached affirmative subject list. This sprint is offline only: no source requests, provider calls, real draft changes, quota changes or publication.

## Source-bound title alternatives

The [V17 trial](112_V17_CURRENT_NOTICES_AND_VALIDATION_HOLD_2026-10-18.md) exposed a broad location quote: the hint enclosed a work-base sentence and a much later location table, overlooking the repeated place name in the title. Location hint V2 retains every original recognised passage and adds `titleAlternatives` with exact title text, its source span and the original `replacesSpan`. `recommendedSpan` replaces V1's `enclosingSpan` in this versioned input contract.

A title alternative requires one exact, single parenthesised repetition of an already recognised simple location-label value, ignoring case and surrounding bracket whitespace. A title alone establishes no hint. Partial names, aliases, multiple/nested brackets, multiline titles, recognised negation/conditional/residence/contact contexts and comma/AND lists abstain. Conflicting recognised labels still produce no recommended range. This is lexical source matching, not geographical classification or independent location verification.

The candidate range retains every mandatory work-base and typed-location passage. It may substitute title evidence only for an eligible generic location-label passage. It is selected only when valid and shorter in characters than the original range, or when the original range cannot fit the evidence bounds. The full contiguous range must stay within selected programme scope, avoid recognised excluded preparation/promotional lines and fit the 4,000-character quote limit. A shorter title window can avoid an excluded line later in the notice; it cannot cross an excluded line or foreign scope between the title and its own work-base evidence.

The recogniser compares bounded alternatives rather than solving global semantic span selection. Original passages remain available, and the model must still return complete, supported evidence. Code does not supply city/province/country values, drop mandatory evidence, infer geography, repair an answer or override a guard.

## Grouping instruction alignment and versions

Extraction already treats an explicit standalone subject acceptance/exclusion as `subjects`. Validation's general instruction to keep qualifications with their listed subjects led the V17 assessor to demand merging the standalone exclusion. A new paragraph clarifies that an otherwise complete qualification can coexist with a separate standalone exclusion. Adjacency alone does not require merging. Required/preferred strength and source applicability must remain intact.

Attached affirmative subject lists still belong in one complete education passage. Route-specific or conditional exclusions must not become unconditional rules, and unclear scope remains a hold. The requirement source policy and all local grouping/evidence guards are unchanged. Model verdict parsing and evidence-integrity checks are unchanged; a negative assessment still holds the worker without retry or override.

Canonical/programme extraction advance to V18, compact extraction stays wire V7 and split metadata advances to V11. Full validation advances to V14, programme validation to V13 and the inherited requirements-only diagnostic to V2. Changed contracts separate cache keys without deleting prior entries or refunding spending. Output schemas, provider selection, fallback/quota policy and token/time caps are unchanged. Validation instructions change only by the grouping clarification; location hint instructions change only for the new source alternatives and recommended range.
