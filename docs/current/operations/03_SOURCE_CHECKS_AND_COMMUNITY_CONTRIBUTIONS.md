# Source checks, corrections and community contributions

Updated: 13 September 2026. Status: operating specification; coverage and actual checks remain to be established. Owner: source operations/founder. Extends [opportunity operations](09_OPPORTUNITY_OPERATIONS.md) and [support](01_SERVICE_OPERATIONS_AND_SUPPORT.md). Engineering enforcement: [implementation contract](../engineering/04_DISCOVERY_COMPANION_IMPLEMENTATION.md).

## 1. Service boundaries and responsibilities

Permitted scraping, direct issuer monitoring and reviewed suggestions feed the same publication process. Scraping remains central; clarification and preparation add value around it. Collecting a lead is not publishing an opportunity. Review all first publications and material changes at M0. Later automation requires parser-specific evidence, bounded permissions and ongoing samples.

Founder initially holds source operator, reviewer and support roles; a separate reviewer is a resource to obtain, not a fictitious employee. Log review actor, method, time, evidence, decision and unresolved questions. Publicly describe actual coverage and response availability. Pause intake or reduce collection scope when backlog exceeds safe capacity; preserve all six category support and disclose real inventory limitations.

## 2. Source register and coverage

For each source record issuer/owner, discovery method, permitted collection basis, public/authenticated scope, original announcement route, application relationship, categories/intakes covered, check schedule, access failures, reuse limits and operator. Never fabricate access permission, affiliation, partnership or accreditation.

The local coverage matrix distinguishes community/area, opportunity category, national/remote reach, active source inventory, last successful review and gaps. A national catalogue can have concentrated first-community depth. Do not imply all local jobs are indexed. Capture residency eligibility separately from place of work/study. Employment stages and internship subtypes remain distinct; no trade-only or entry-level-only restriction.

Publish concise attribution and source links with only permitted necessary excerpts. Store original/discovery source and destination separately. When an aggregator reveals the issuer, move ongoing checks to the permitted primary route where practical while preserving discovery provenance. Remove content or change collection when rights/access conditions require it; no rotating-identity evasion.

## 3. Review workflow

1. Validate public-link/source scope and isolate unsafe or sensitive submissions. Use the approved retrieval environment, not a credentialed personal session.
2. Identify issuer and candidate duplicate/intake. Preserve separate references, locations and intakes where appropriate.
3. Find the primary notice or another appropriate independently corroborated source. Social-only announcements and small issuers need review, not automatic rejection as fraud.
4. Establish the actual application route, including a third-party ATS if the issuer directs users there. Record the relationship evidence. A familiar logo/domain alone is insufficient.
5. Review material facts: category/subtype, requirements and their logical relationships, residence/location, deadline, compensation/funding, documents and delivery constraints.
6. Review the explanation against the original. Remove invented thresholds, inferred qualification equivalences and unsupported reassurance. Preserve unresolved facts explicitly.
7. Publish an approved version with source/check summaries and a recheck schedule, or leave unresolved with a reason. A live URL alone does not prove an open intake.
8. Record time/cost and sample decisions during review meetings. Route errors into corrective action and parser/template improvements.

Unknown nonessential fields can remain unknown. Identity/destination ambiguity that prevents an appropriate application handoff cannot be concealed behind a reassuring badge. An accessible official notice with an unspecified deadline may be shown with that limitation; an unresolved alleged opportunity need not be published to fill a category.

## 4. Check schedule and changes

Set source-specific review intervals before launch based on deadline proximity, source reliability, volatility and available capacity. Record dueAt and overdue status. Prioritise material destination/eligibility changes and approaching known deadlines. A check failure retains the last successful check timestamp and a recheck-needed state; never refresh the timestamp without doing the check.

Material changes include required qualification/marks, document constraints, deadline, location/residency, compensation terms, application destination and withdrawal. Store a version diff and invalidate relevant public caches/indexes. Saved browser users see changes on revisit; do not promise push updates in M0. Old forwarded messages cannot be recalled, so the current canonical landing page must make closure/change visible.

Handle stale source text, time zones and date-only deadlines explicitly. Do not invent midnight or a false countdown. Keep closed intake records distinct from a later reopening/new intake. Mistaken merges and corrections must be reversible with stable public links.

## 5. Reports and clarification

M0 report reasons: looks suspicious, asks for money, incorrect information, broken destination, closed opportunity, unclear requirement. Bound any optional text; tell users not to send ID documents or unrelated private material. Acknowledgement confirms receipt, not that the allegation is true or resolved.

| Triage class | Operator action | Public behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Credible serious safety, credential theft or payment concern | One report can trigger urgent review; restrict implicated destination/record while investigating when warranted; route to incident handling | Neutral under-review state and safe fallback; do not accuse an issuer without evidence |
| Material eligibility/deadline/destination error | Review against the source, correct or clarify, update version and affected surfaces | Visible current facts and material-change note |
| Broken link/recheck failure | Check source/destination relationship and retry within policy | Distinguish temporary unavailability from closure |
| Ambiguous requirement | Seek clarification through an authorised channel when operationally permitted, or mark unresolved | Attribute any answer and retain source wording |
| Spam/coordinated reporting | Rate-limit, group related reports and inspect evidence | Report volume alone does not establish fraud or auto-delete a valid listing |

Do not require two reports before looking at a serious concern. Do not automatically remove listings solely because two browser identities report them. Set severity-based response targets and actual coverage hours before publication; this plan invents no 24/7 response commitment.

A clarification distinguishes source correction, authorised issuer response, operator explanation and community experience. A user saying "I was accepted with these marks" does not change the current published eligibility rule. External contact by agents requires the founder's explicit authorisation; these procedures are not an instruction to send messages now.

## 6. Free service and accurate safety language

Skilved consumer access, matching, preparation, assistance and own-record access remain free. Proposed interface copy: "Skilved does not charge applicants. Asked to pay someone in connection with this opportunity? Pause and report the request so it can be checked." Final wording must reflect actual service scope and legal review.

Do not publish "all fees everywhere are illegal", infer fraud from any incidental education cost, or promise every listed opportunity is safe. Distinguish source-stated tuition/programme expenses from suspicious recruitment/payment demands without normalising a demand for employment placement. Section 15's employment-service fee prohibition and exception mechanism require accurate contextual treatment; use the [legal drafting pack](../legal/01_PUBLIC_NOTICE_AND_TERMS_DRAFTING_PACK.md) and its source register.

## 7. Suggestions and employer intake

Public link suggestions are an M0 review queue with private receipts, no applicant account requirement and no automatic public authorship. Receipt states and retention follow engineering. Unsupported/private routes can be declined honestly; screenshots and personal documents wait for later controls. Do not promise a completed investigation before the closing date.

A richer employer/provider submission form is a post-M0 option. Record official source, issuer, category, role of submitter and minimally necessary business contact; independently establish authority where needed before assigning a representative badge. Payment or a submission cannot purchase trust status. Reuse the source queue rather than creating an unreviewed publishing channel.

## 8. Capacity, costs and evidence

Track reviewed new listings, material changes, source failures, unresolved leads, reports by severity, oldest backlog age, human minutes, model/fetch cost and correction turnaround. Record actual counts and coverage windows. Launch readiness requires a staffed review process, available pause/withhold controls and a source inventory the founder can maintain.

Run a weekly source-quality sample across all six categories and higher-risk changes during initial operation; adjust frequency from measured errors and workload. This is an operating proposal, not evidence of completed reviews. Analyse missed issues and unnecessary removals. Quality failures may require pausing a source/parser before adding inventory. No fixed nationwide source count or moderation headcount is assumed.

Use [DCF tests](../quality/02_DISCOVERY_COMPANION_ACCEPTANCE.md) and [M0 delivery](../planning/03_M0_COMPANION_DELIVERY_PLAN.md). Institutional procurement and deeper INV research remain after launch; source and safety operations for the launched feed are ongoing delivery obligations.
