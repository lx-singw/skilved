## Cumulative qualifications retain their scope

The [captured 3.1 answer](100_GEMINI31_HTTP_TIMING_AND_ALPLA_FIDELITY_2026-10-18.md) changed a school qualification plus a minimum technical qualification into alternatives. The source policy now recognises a deliberately narrow closed profile: one short school qualification followed by one explicitly minimum N-certificate qualification, with only recognised independent subject, results-document, licence or introduction lines.

The policy exposes the original qualification spans as cumulative. Their separate education items must remain unconditionally required through ALL, including nested ALL groups; ANY and IF branches cannot weaken them. A document passage's own internal OR stays verbatim within that document item. A complete unknown passage remains a hold, and cannot excuse a partial confident qualification branch.

Explicit alternative/conditional wording, unknown continuations, blank separators, repeated qualification wording, preferred/document sections and unbounded sections do not receive this conjunction hint. Other layouts still require source and semantic review. This is not a rule that every list of qualifications must be ALL.

Standalone recognised Mathematics/Mathematical Literacy not applicable/not accepted exclusions now receive the existing subjects kind check. Qualification-attached subjects remain in their complete education passage.
