## Shared content checks

The adapter accepts one visible `<main>` or `role="main"`. Without either, it requires a prose link in a paragraph associated with a careers H1 through a content container. Bare page-wide links and an uncontained title are insufficient. Repeated normalized identical careers titles are permitted; conflicting careers titles and multiple visible main regions remain unsupported. Content headers within main remain supported.

Navigation, menus, banners, sidebars, footers, dialogs, breadcrumbs and cookie/consent regions are excluded using semantic tags, roles and bounded common chrome tokens. Scripts, forms, hidden content and inline visibility rules remain excluded. Base-href changes are rejected, including within hidden subtrees. The input bound remains 500,000 bytes, with 20,000 visited nodes, depth 100 and a shared 200,000-step text-work budget. Exceeding a bound rejects the result rather than returning partially collected authority. This is static supported-markup analysis, not computed browser visibility or universal CMS support.

Warning labels and link-local warning sentences cannot qualify. A remote paragraph sentence containing a plain negation may be distinguished from an explicit affirmative application-through-the-link sentence. Strong warning directives, contradictory portal/site descriptions and ambiguous text remain held. Thus an unrelated unsolicited-offer/payment disclaimer need not suppress a genuine application link. This is a bounded text rule, not an AI classification or a general understanding of negation. A warning covering the target root/path/vacancy defeats a narrower positive link, even when that warning URL itself could not grant delegation.

## Scope and versioning

Root delegation requires all of the following:

- A current reviewed parent and its fixed corporate careers page.
- An explicit safe HTTPS link to the exact candidate recruitment host, without a query or fragment.
- A corporate host that is its registrable apex or `www` host.
- A linked strict subdomain of that same registrable domain, distinct from its `www` host.
- ICANN boundaries with private public suffixes considered; private hosting/platform roots and unknown suffixes are refused.

Public-suffix interpretation uses pinned `tldts` 7.4.19, with its locked core dependency, and `allowPrivateDomains: true`; see the [library's primary documentation](https://github.com/remusao/tldts). No recruitment hostname, tenant or corporate domain is constructed from a name/email/job ID. Non-root shared ATS paths retain their explicit path-prefix requirements and narrowest-linked-scope selection.

New proofs use `corporate_delegation_v2` with `scopeKind: linked_path` or `corporate_subdomain`. Root proofs are rechecked against the corporate domain boundary on reading. V1 path proofs remain readable with their original JSON/bindings and can be reused while valid. V1 root proofs and missing/mismatched V2 scope declarations are invalid. Existing current proof IDs, immutable versions, source settings, grants and spend are not migrated or reset.

Parent revision/hash, exact careers URL, issuer names/aliases, snapshot, source/revision, maximum six-hour lifetime and parent expiry remain bound. Revocation and changes are checked before effects and cached use. A delegated root authorizes only portal acquisition on that host; exact original vacancy identity/URL/reference/deadline checks still follow. It grants no email recipients, publication privilege or exemption from robots, pinned DNS/TLS or optional employer-crawl policy.
