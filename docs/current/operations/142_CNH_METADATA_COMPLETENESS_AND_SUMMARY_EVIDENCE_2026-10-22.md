## Extraction changes

The metadata policy supplies exact source passages for narrowly recognized forms. Explicit Reference and Requisition ID/number labels bind their complete label/value passage to intake, including next-line identifiers. Missing identifiers, partial identifiers, conflicting recognized codes and evidence that drops the label fail. An application subject is not inferred to be a requisition.

An explicit invitation to unemployed youth must retain that audience in the summary value and cite the entire invitation. Presence only in a quote is insufficient. This invitation does not establish a numeric age, citizenship, residence or mandatory eligibility rule; code does not create requirement items from it.

Within a bounded What We Offer section, a recognized regional comprehensive benefits introduction followed by a closed Retirement benefits / Medical aid list must retain the regional scope and listed benefits. Compensation uses kind other with null amount, maximum and currency and an unstated period. Its coverage retains the scope and benefit wording, and its evidence includes the full introduction and list. Dropping the scope, citing only the list, inventing an amount or adding a guarantee fails. This records corporate regional wording, not verified applicability to every vacancy. Conditional, unfamiliar or unbounded benefit layouts remain for semantic review.

The summary instruction requires every claim to be supported by its own contiguous passage. A local check catches exact city/province names from returned location metadata, and recognized reference codes, repeated in a summary without those terms in its quote. Other fields need not be repeated in the summary. This is a bounded evidence check: it does not establish arbitrary trade paraphrases, every geographic alias, benefit applicability or general semantic correctness. The existing independent validation stage remains required.

Publisher preparation and promotional text, and lines outside a selected programme, do not supply these metadata obligations. Existing qualitative-pay and maximum-duration hints now also respect selected programme scope. Recognized forms produce hints or rejection; no returned field, quote or span is repaired.
