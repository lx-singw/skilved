## Location evidence checks

A shared `assertLocationEvidence` check runs in canonical mapping for ordinary, split-stage and programme extraction. Each stated city and province must occur as a complete normalised phrase in the location quote. Case and punctuation normalisation allow a written province such as KwaZulu-Natal to match its canonical code. A city cannot supply an unstated province. A country needs its explicit code or, for ZA, the written country name South Africa. No place is supplied from geographical knowledge.

Attendance needs a narrow affirmative Attendance, Work arrangement or Work mode label with a single recognised value. Coverage similarly needs a recognised coverage/scope label. A Locations/All provinces field, including the two-line table format, explicitly supports national coverage without supplying a country. A work location does not establish local coverage or in-person attendance. Negations, alternatives, conflicting labels, local-residence rules and remote-control duties do not establish these values. Relocation must copy a complete source line about relocation rather than inventing or paraphrasing a policy.

Unsupported values fail as `AI_LOCATION_EVIDENCE_UNRESOLVED`; the mapper does not remove or repair them. This conservative check supports narrow label formats and is not comprehensive semantic verification. Legitimate arrangements written in other forms can require review; recognising a place name in a quote does not independently authenticate the employer or destination.
