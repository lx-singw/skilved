## Retain narrow application and deadline evidence

The initial Zurel parse misses two visible formats. A private replay after the parsing changes recovers both without changing the original source text:

- A bare email on the next line is recognised only when an immediately preceding `How to apply` heading (optionally numbered) is followed by the exact `Send an email to` instruction and a single valid email line. Original evidence retains all three contiguous lines. Unlabelled emails, enquiry/form-request instructions, prohibitions, intervening text, multiple recipients and URLs do not satisfy this new pattern.
- An optional full weekday before a closing date is retained in the original evidence. The calendar date is parsed normally, and a conflicting weekday or invalid date stays unresolved. Unstated time and timezone remain unknown.

The new email candidate is evidence for review, not permission to send a message, a mailbox-deliverability check or employer verification.

## Hold shared-email subjects before AI and publication

Counting one email does not prove there is only one intake. A narrow source pattern—`Use the correct subject line:` followed by distinct matching `Trade — if applying for Trade` lines—adds `programme_subject_scope_review_required` to the candidate's issues. Zurel matches this pattern.

Ordinary preflight and publication reject this unresolved scope as `NOTICE_PROGRAMME_SCOPE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, even with a registered recipient or a model-supported record. A shadow worker also saves a source-bound programme plan when this flag is present. With programme extraction enabled, Zurel's unsupported layout stops as `PROGRAMME_LAYOUT_UNSUPPORTED` before any model work. Its two trade subjects cannot silently enter the ordinary single-notice path just because they share an email.

This is a guard for the observed explicit format, not a general detector of every multi-trade layout. The current planner still requires separately labelled trades, explicit references and separately scoped written application emails. It does not invent reference numbers or infer trade-specific qualification subjects from Zurel's wording.
