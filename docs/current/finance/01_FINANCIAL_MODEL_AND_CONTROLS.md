## Separate four questions

1. What does the service cost to operate at a measured workload?
2. What cash can the founder safely contribute after actual essentials and reserves?
3. Will a customer pay enough for a useful outcome?
4. Can the business deliver that outcome repeatedly with a positive contribution after labour?

The founder's apprenticeship provides accommodation and transport and a stated R3,000 stipend. Actual food, data, personal obligations and savings needs are unknown. Therefore this plan does not allocate an invented disposable amount or treat an earlier suggested R200 as a confirmed cap. Maintain private household calculations outside the public repository.

## Cash worksheet structure

| Line | Calculation / evidence |
|---|---|
| Opening business cash | Reconciled actual balance |
| Founder contribution | Explicitly affordable amount, recorded as appropriate funding type |
| Customer cash received | Cleared payments; separate deposits and refundable obligations |
| Committed outflows | Hosting, domains, tools, delivery expenses and agreed contracts |
| Expected variable outflows | Measured workload × current provider/SKU rate plus contingency |
| Professional/filing costs | Actual quotes and applicable obligations |
| Tax provision | Based on actual entity/activity and qualified advice; no invented rate here |
| Closing available cash | Opening + receipts − payments − amounts reserved for obligations |
| Forward coverage | Available unrestricted cash divided by estimated net cash burn, where burn is positive |

Update weekly during initial operation and reconcile to invoices/bank records monthly. Promotional credits are a temporary reduction with expiry and eligibility; they are not recurring revenue. Foreign-currency cost scenarios require exchange-rate and tax assumptions clearly identified.

## Unit economics

For a customer cohort or service: contribution = cash-recognised service value under the adopted accounting treatment minus direct provider charges, payment fees, external delivery costs and a transparent labour allowance. Track operating cash separately from accounting recognition. Under the confirmed free-consumer model, institutional revenue or other authorised funding must cover consumer operating costs as well as paid delivery costs.

The existing experimental R1,500 pilot has an illustrative calculation: R150 direct expense and eight hours valued at R100/hour leave R550 before tax and other overhead. At sixteen hours the same assumptions produce a R250 loss. These are sensitivity examples, not observed profitability. Log actual time and cost before expanding the offer.

Measure cost per accepted source record, useful preparation workspace, completed background check, active record, paying organisation and supported learner. Do not optimise cost per token while ignoring manual review or failed user tasks.

## Approval and payment controls

Before a commitment, record provider/customer, purpose, amount/currency, recurring term, renewal, cancellation, authorised funds and owner. Do not provision uncapped variable work because the founder requested uncapped ambition. Configure available alerts, quotas and eligible provider controls while recognising delays and excluded charges. Review invoices against usage and authorised commitments.

Keep invoice numbering, receipts, expenses, refunds and receivables in a private financial ledger. Use the actual contracting identity and tax treatment. Deposits fund delivery obligations; avoid using them as unrestricted personal spending. Any transfer between founder and business needs a clear classification and supporting record.

## Growth and funding decision

Seek external capital only with a specific use, milestone, runway and ownership consequence. Revenue, grants, paid service work and equity have different conditions. A growing company valuation is not liquid personal wealth. Sustainable founder income requires actual cash-generating capacity and appropriate remuneration/distribution decisions, not merely a large user count.

Revisit the model after the first actual invoice, the first real hosting month, ten delivered workspaces and each material workload change. Numerical cloud scenarios remain in the linked cost document to prevent conflicting duplicate models.
