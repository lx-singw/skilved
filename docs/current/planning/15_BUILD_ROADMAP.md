## Product destination

Skilved connects a portable career record to useful opportunities, sustained personal assistance and people who help resolve obstacles. Work and learning create attributable evidence. Evidence supports applications, assessment and recognition. Outcomes lead to the next period of work, learning or support. The person can carry their record across institutions.

The public catalogue includes bursaries, learnerships, apprenticeships, internships, graduate programmes and jobs. A user can bring an external opportunity, prepare an application and export selected evidence without requiring the opportunity holder to join Skilved.

The full portfolio includes practical logs, team contributions, references, assessed challenges, structured help, supervised school participation, coordinator workflows, referrals, programme progress, RPL preparation, authorised credentials, supported application adapters, evidence-informed guidance, carefully governed aggregate insights and international expansion. Each has a home below; none is claimed as working simply because it appears in a diagram.

## How to read the plan

- **R0–R5** are cumulative product releases. A later release includes the earlier useful capabilities.
- **S01–S35** are ordered delivery work packages, scheduled through iterative sprints. A work package may span more than one two-week iteration.
- Effort includes implementation, tests, source curation, user research, documentation, release work and initial sales/support relevant to the package. Do not add separate agent estimates a second time.
- External waiting time, partner procurement, seasonal opportunity supply and sustained outcome observation are separate from hands-on effort.
- Targets are proposed acceptance thresholds or experiments, not evidence of competitor weakness or guaranteed business outcomes.
- Funding can accelerate capacity only when contributors, access and resources are actually available. More agents do not automatically make customer decisions or independent reviews faster.

## R0 — Working foundation

**Objective:** establish what works and make future implementation trustworthy.

Deliver reproducible local build instructions, a baseline of tests/failures, six-category contracts, real owner-aware persistence and authentication, protected roles, private worker invocation and explicit demo isolation. Inventory cloud resources read-only before altering or provisioning them. Preserve the existing UI where useful.

Repository risks already observed include mock listings, in-memory demo identity, placeholder database helpers, fabricated verification, publicly invokable generic worker infrastructure and insufficiently protected role/screenshot rules. R0 acceptance requires resolving or disabling these paths, not merely adding documentation.

**Gate:** a second local account cannot read another's private record or assign privileged roles; seeded examples are unmistakably synthetic; the baseline and tests are reproducible. No external credential check is represented as completed without a real receipt.

**Owner:** founder/implementer. Independent security review is a resource to arrange where possible, not an assumed team member.

## R1 — Supervised alpha

**Objective:** prove the connected product journey with apprentice peers and school leavers.

Build the permitted source register and a small real catalogue, source-backed opportunity details, reviewed extraction, category-specific requirements, user-imported links, editable career records, selected document/evidence packs and external application handoff. Include a basic public-facing profile/export that contains only selected career information. Record actual feedback from supervised users.

Cover all six category paths in fixtures and representative end-to-end trials. Source quantity is a planning variable, not a reason to fill gaps with expired or fabricated listings. Where seasonal supply is limited, show that limitation and distinguish it from missing product support.

**Gate:** a user completes a real preparation task without invented qualifications, deadlines or submission claims; a recipient can interpret a selected pack; an operator can correct a listing. A small useful product exists even before a partner contract.

## R2 — Expanded consumer release

**Objective:** deliver the complete first consumer journey reliably.

Add durable personal goals/tasks, reviewed CV/evidence import, next-step recommendations, change/deadline handling, opt-in notifications, cancellation, outcome records, accessibility, mobile performance, account recovery, deletion/export and routine operations. Browse all six categories publicly; saving, private preparation and continuing work use authenticated ownership. Add internal queues for sources, tasks, reports and support.

Under-18 audiences remain in the strategy. Public browsing can be available without private profile collection. Under-18 records/contact/social features require the specific privacy, supervision and safeguarding gate; use an adult supervised alpha if those arrangements are not ready. Do not quietly delete school learners from the roadmap.

**Gate:** source freshness, access control, durable execution, correction/reporting, restore and mobile checks pass. Publish scope and source coverage honestly. Launch requires usable representative inventory across the promised categories, with clearly explained seasonal limitations; six empty navigation tabs are not a successful launch.

**Commercial parallel:** test the existing fixed-scope service with one willing coordinator. Software billing or mandatory organisation onboarding is not a prerequisite for public user value.

## R3 — Useful network and paid workflow

**Objective:** make human contribution improve outcomes and test a repeatable buyer workflow.

Deliver invited cohorts, structured help, narrowly scoped referee confirmations, collaborative projects with individual credit, assessor feedback and limited coordinator reporting. Test evidence packs with real recipients. Add organisation onboarding, permissions and simple billing only for a validated offer. Test authentic sharing and a showcase using consented work.

Do not introduce an open feed merely to increase time-on-site. The social unit is a useful request, contribution or next step. A network effect is supported when another relevant participant improves utility, not when registered accounts increase.

**Gate:** at least one repeated organisational workflow has demonstrated value, support is operable and a real payment/renewal experiment has an outcome. If no buyer emerges, revise the commercial hypothesis rather than automatically proceed to enterprise features.

## R4 — Institutional continuity

**Objective:** allow useful evidence and support to survive institutional transitions.

Build practical activity/logbook templates, assessment criteria, batch acknowledgements, issuer-specific evidence, programme onboarding/check-ins/exit packs, handover/referral acceptance, RPL preparation with authorised providers, and actual credential integrations requested by participating organisations. Add retention/deletion policies, audit exports and accessibility/localisation informed by usage.

MyMzansi, SAQA, NAMB, QCTO, SETAs and standards-based records are dependency-specific possibilities. Do not fabricate an API, claim recognition or require a government integration to deliver earlier releases.

**Gate:** receiving organisations use records for a defined purpose; independent recipients can distinguish claims and provenance; programme partners can sustain the workflow; any official integration is authorised, tested and revocable. Database/search architecture is reassessed before institutional complexity grows.

## R5 — Evidence-led expansion

**Objective:** expand capabilities and geography only where data, economics and partnerships support them.

Potential work includes supported application adapters, evidence-informed pathway comparisons, aggregate skills reports, carefully governed employer feedback, collective insights, credential interoperability at scale, country localisation and sector extensions. Preserve the gig/business-administration idea as a separate later experiment with its own buyer and economics.

Population-level claims need population-relevant evidence, not platform inventory. Career predictions need validation and uncertainty handling. Public organisational assessments need suitable evidence, correction and dispute processes. An international launch needs local source access, language, support, legal and market work.

**Gate:** each workstream has its own customer, justified data, operating owner and sustainable costs. The organisation may choose only the valuable workstreams; the plan does not require executing weak ideas for completeness.

## Critical path and parallel work

`Baseline → ownership/contracts → source-to-record → preparation/evidence → durable follow-through → public release controls`

The path above describes the expanded product. M0 uses the earlier discovery path in the 13 September revision. Conduct launch-specific observation and source checks during M0; begin the broader recipient/buyer investigations after M0. Their gates remain before institutional commitments. Architecture decisions, edits to shared contracts and approvals remain coordinated.

Partner credentials, WhatsApp automation and broad ATS submission are not on the R2 critical path. A source that cannot be accessed permissibly is replaced or handled through another allowed route. A delayed integration does not block every category.

| Trigger | Resource to consider | Evidence before commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Repeated security-sensitive implementation | Independent reviewer or experienced engineer | Defined review surface and findings/remediation plan |
| Source/support queue exceeds sustainable capacity | Part-time operations help | Measured queue volume, response times and funding |

| Localisation or school programme grows | Local coordinator/safeguarding owner | Actual participating community and responsibilities |
| Data/analytics products are requested | Domain analyst/research collaborator | Appropriate data and a buyer/research question |

Paid services, grants, credits and investment are possible resources. None is a dependency that is presumed to arrive on a particular day. Grants and credits require eligibility and expiry checks. Revenue, profit, cash and owner income are distinct.

## Release evidence and change control

Each release folder or issue set must link: accepted requirements, tests, anonymised usability observations, source-quality sample, cost sample, known limitations, deployment/rollback evidence and unresolved dependencies. An untested mock does not satisfy the gate. An operational failure reopens the relevant gate.

At each two-week review: compare planned and actual effort; inspect user progress; record harmful or incorrect outputs; review costs; choose the next useful package; update ranges. When new scope is added, show what moves or what additional resources are needed. Ambition is retained through transparent scheduling, not hidden overtime.
