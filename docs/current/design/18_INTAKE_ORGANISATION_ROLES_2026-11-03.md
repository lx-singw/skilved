# Intake and organisation role file

Implemented: 3 November 2026.

The detail page now has a category-specific intake disclosure with an inventory of stated and unresolved role entries. It opens a source-linked file containing the intake/reference value, notice issuer and individual organisation role records. The summary counts recorded entries rather than unique organisations, people or verified relationships. Repeated names in different roles remain separate, including independently recorded issuer facts.

Each of the five stated organisation roles has its own icon, label and short explanation: issuer, employer or host, training provider, qualification issuer and oversight body. Ambiguous, conflicting and absent records retain their own status and wording without an inferred role or organisation name. Missing role information has a specific explanation rather than an instruction to read unavailable wording. No accreditation, endorsement or overall verification badge is inferred.

The stated intake value is readable independently of its original wording. Native source disclosures retain full wording, line breaks and only that fact's cited public references. Missing wording and missing references are labelled separately for unresolved facts; evidence is never borrowed from another role or the notice issuer. The controls have distinct accessible names for the intake, issuer and each ordinal role entry. Source text, organisation names and reference labels are escaped and wrap in full without truncation.

Internship context retains student/WIL, graduate, other-stated and unknown routes with the supplied route wording and its existing eligibility clarification. Other categories do not receive an internship route by inference. The public read and projection boundaries, requirements, conditions, saved progress and application handoff are unchanged.

The disclosure now belongs to OpportunityContext itself, avoiding a second wrapper in the page. The existing context-heading fragment targets its visible native summary; the inner section is labelled by context-file-title. This places a keyboard-ready opening control at the destination on direct navigation and reload, rather than targeting a hidden heading and relying on the browser to mutate open before hydration. The final implementation needs no hydration-warning suppression or client-side accordion state.

Verification passed: all 269 web tests, typecheck, lint and production build. Eight new tests cover every category, up to twenty separate role records, repeated names, stated and unresolved inventory, intake value versus original wording, all five roles with independent citations, missing evidence, long Unicode text and escaped markup, and real public projection of pending facts. Existing conditions tests retain role/citation and privacy assertions with the new markup; pending role names are checked within their role section while the separately public issuer remains visible. The full-detail source test retains its positive and withheld/unavailable boundaries against the new section title. The strengthened pending-role assertion also passed a focused rerun.

Browser review used the existing fictional graduate programme plus an ignored display-only role sample. Direct fragment navigation and reload landed on the visible summary at approximately 205 pixels from the viewport top; Enter opened it. Source disclosures worked from the keyboard, and a provider entry showed its own provider reference. The graduate-category back-to-results link retained its category query and card anchor. The display-only sample covered all five roles, repeated long multilingual names, a conflicting intake and two unresolved entries.

At a measured 389 CSS-pixel viewport, document scroll width equalled the 370-pixel client width. All seven sample role entries and an open provider source disclosure had no horizontal overflow; the eight source-disclosure controls measured 44 pixels high. The temporary viewport override was reset. Desktop layout was visually reviewed and a complete marked fictional sample screenshot saved outside the repository. Initial screenshot capture failed; full-page capture succeeded after the preview was shown. A later viewport capture was partial, so the complete sample is the reliable image for review.

The sample route exists only in ignored .artifacts files and creates display data from a public fictional record without updating that record. No source visit, application, save/stage change, report or suggestion was submitted during browser review. Source linkage remains a record of the public evidence, not proof that a source or organisation has been independently verified. Production deployment and broader user research remain separate.
