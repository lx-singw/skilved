# Practical conditions file

Implemented: 4 November 2026.

The practical conditions section now uses a four-part notice file: location and attendance, residence, experience, and duration/other arrangements. Six category-specific headings and the existing category colours introduce the setting. Numbered entries, distinct icons and native source drawers follow the pay/support file's visual language without combining separate facts into a single verdict.

Location preserves coverage and attendance as separate recorded fields. Places appear individually with city/locality, a readable province label and the recorded country code. Missing locality, province or country remains explicit, including when a province is recorded but no country is given. No country, residence eligibility, relocation support, commute estimate or map coordinate is inferred. A remote or national arrangement may have no individual places; that absence does not erase its recorded coverage/attendance. Relocation remains its own complete wording.

Residence conditions stay separate from workplaces. Experience and practical arrangements display their stated value while retaining their original source wording in the corresponding drawer. Uncertain facts keep distinct not-stated, ambiguous and conflicting states, without promoting residual structured values. Long/multiline conditions are not shortened or parsed into new entry requirements, employment terms or minimum experience claims.

The public contract already requires a stated fact to have a value, original wording and citations. The missing-wording fallback in the presentation is defensive; it is not a newly accepted public fact or evidence of a published-data defect. Tests explicitly reject that malformed projection before exercising the component fallback separately. Validation and publication boundaries were not loosened.

Each source drawer resolves only that fact's unique available references, keeps its original wording complete and identifies missing wording/references separately. Links show source hostnames and open separately. The evidence checks remain a separate destination. Source counts and stated labels do not imply issuer verification, applicant eligibility or confirmed availability.

The existing `conditions-heading` remains a visible keyboard target. New location, residence, experience and arrangement headings are also visible and focusable outside closed drawers. Overview questions and category-specific reading links now point to the corresponding subsection. Native fragments preserve the existing detail page context and do not open drawers, record a review or change personal progress automatically.
