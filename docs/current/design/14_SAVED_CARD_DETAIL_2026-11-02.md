# Saved cards and personal history

Implemented: 2 November 2026.

Saved opportunity cards now carry two category-specific facts from the same reading lens used in the feed and detail workspace. Bursaries show funding and study fields; jobs show pay and experience; apprenticeships show trade and support; learnerships show the learning arrangement and support; internships show their placement route and field; graduate programmes show fields and education instructions. Each preview also retains the setting, attendance and closing information.

The preview comes from the existing validated, no-store public read. No public facts, source wording or application destination are added to local progress storage. Refresh still clears the previous summary before a new response; obsolete responses and disposed readers cannot restore it. Restricted, missing and unavailable responses contain no preview. Existing identity, revision and closed-intake guards continue to choose the primary action.

Long fact values use the existing native full-wording disclosure, now shared with Saved. Original text stays complete and escaped. Education summaries preserve instruction counts and connected alternatives rather than deciding eligibility. Missing, ambiguous and conflicting facts retain distinct meanings. Closing disagreements no longer collapse into the same generic clarification label; any supplied wording to clarify remains visible without inventing a date, time, timezone or countdown.

Personal stage controls now have stage-specific context and a separate last-local-update timestamp. History displays the original save date, latest local update, recorded route action and any earlier personal Applied report. Earlier report times remain visible after changing stage; they do not select or imply the current stage. The view explains that Applied is a personal report, a route action does not confirm that a destination loaded or a submission occurred, and displayed personal times use the browser's local timezone. The same history presentation is used on opportunity details.

Compact changed-detail notices keep one primary card action instead of duplicating the review link. Cross-reference identity explanations and their canonical listing links remain explicit; this sprint introduces no automatic acknowledgements, stage matches or personal writes on reading.
