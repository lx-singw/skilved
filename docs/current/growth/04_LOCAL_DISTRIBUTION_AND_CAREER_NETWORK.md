# Local distribution and the future career network

Updated: 13 September 2026. Status: accepted staged direction; acquisition, retention, partnerships and network effects remain hypotheses. Owner: founder/product/growth. Read with [M0](../product/03_DISCOVERY_FIRST_MVP.md), [experience](../design/05_OPPORTUNITY_COMPANION_EXPERIENCE.md), [institutional model](../commercial/03_TVET_SDF_AND_EMPLOYER_BUSINESS_MODEL.md) and [investigations](../research/02_STRATEGIC_INVESTIGATIONS_AND_PRODUCT_OPTIONS.md).

## 1. Audience and positioning

"Skilved helps people starting their careers in South Africa make sense of work and study opportunities—with clear sources, practical next steps and support that grows with them."

The first reachable audience is apprentice peers and school leavers. This guides language, first research participants, source depth and distribution effort. It does not remove other users, non-entry-level jobs, graduate internships, national bursaries, remote options or any of the six categories. High-school users remain part of the vision under feature-specific age and safeguarding controls.

Hyperlocal value means reliable information about actual places, residence conditions, relevant providers, programme routes and community needs. A location filter or local branding alone is no competitive defence. Preserve user-controlled widening and future mobility; no hidden locality exclusion. No community, school, TVET or WhatsApp-group partnership is claimed until agreed.

## 2. Two useful acquisition paths

Path A: permitted scraping/direct sources → reviewed public opportunity → someone browses → voluntarily shares a current Skilved link → a recipient gets value without a registration wall. Path B: someone encounters an opportunity elsewhere → brings a supported public link → Skilved locates an existing reviewed record or investigates the source → the person understands the next step. Neither path replaces the main feed.

Prepare accurate share text, modest previews, stable canonical pages and maintained thematic collections. Source-based shelves can be useful before any audience exists. Social platforms remain distribution channels; Skilved need not own every conversation to help people. Outbound contact, paid promotion and group posting require actual authorisation; this plan performs or authorises no unsolicited messages, scraping of private groups or contact-book upload.

Examples of collections: school-leaver pathways, bursaries by field, apprenticeship routes, WIL opportunities where academic approval is relevant. Collection inclusion has published editorial criteria and current source review. Label editor's picks as editorial, never fake popularity. Reuse catalogue records rather than copying stale descriptions into separate collection databases.

## 3. Proposed initial distribution work

First learn whether reachable people understand and share the detail page. With willing community administrators, later test sharing into an existing group and compare useful visits, not only member count. Record channel, permission, content/as-of date, audience relevance and actual outcomes without collecting member lists.

Ask what a recipient needs to trust a shared listing and what made the sender choose it. Do not pressure people to perform a share during usability testing and count it as organic behaviour. Referrals should be optional, with no access lock, application advantage, forced invitation quota or reward for spam.

Search discovery and returning direct visits are additional channels to measure; no guaranteed indexing, rankings or traffic. Partner channels may distribute useful listings while preserving original attribution and application routes. Budget and staff time for source care and support rise with distribution; growth that overwhelms review is not success.

## 4. Staged social product

| Stage / portfolio home | Capability | Evidence and operating gate |
|---|---|---|
| M0: useful individually | Browse, source checks, local saved progress, share, suggest and report | Works without another registered person; reviewed publication and useful first task |
| Post-M0 / R3: better with others | Reviewed clarifications, shared collections, invited cohorts, mentors and structured help | Repeated help need observed, named moderation owner, permissions/report/block controls and appropriate school safeguards |
| R3–R4+: purposeful career network | Selected career profiles, projects, contribution credit, referrals, programme communities and employer participation | Genuine useful interactions, provenance, consent, dispute handling, sustainable operations and role/identity boundaries |
| Later conditional expansion | Wider public discovery of people/groups, following, comments, messaging and additional network interactions | Feature-specific demand, abuse/impersonation controls, safe contact settings, moderation capacity and re-estimated scope |

The social network is retained, not permanently rejected. Some users want social connection and others want a quick task; ordinary discovery must remain useful to both. Real user questions or existing group activity can justify an invited Skilved group. Empty-room risk is addressed through purposeful initial activity, not fake posts, profiles, comments or testimonials.

Distinguish personal experience, operator explanation, authorised issuer clarification, supervisor observation and assessed/issued evidence. Popularity cannot validate a credential or change eligibility. Community participation never grants blanket access to private career files. Compulsory institutional usage is not evidence of voluntary consumer retention.

## 5. Trending and company discovery

Keep trending as a post-M0 experiment after source clarity, continuity and sharing. Start with genuine activity on Skilved, a declared window, adequate support, bot/staff/test exclusion, duplicate prevention, manipulation review and correction/rollback. A possible save-based signal is an experiment; no fixed formula or ten-browser threshold is adopted as a product guarantee. Choose thresholds before evaluating results, using measured sample reliability and privacy needs. If support is inadequate, retain chronological/editorial shelves.

Provide a chronological alternative, maintain user filters and inspect whether exposure creates self-reinforcing popularity that hides lesser-known issuers. Views, share intents and browser identities are not applicants or verified people. Popularity is not suitability, opportunity safety, labour-market demand or chance of selection. Sponsorship cannot secretly alter an organic trend result.

Company pages initially show source-backed open listings found by Skilved, categories and check times. They do not rank employer quality or hiring speed. Later company following and new-listing alerts require reliable identity matching, version history and opt-in delivery. Broad employer outcome comparisons remain PRD-058 gated work.

## 6. Measure usefulness and continuity

For M0, evaluate source/requirement comprehension in observed sessions and useful actions in privacy-appropriate aggregate events. Saves, route opens and self-reported applications are separate. Where lawful measurement cannot identify repeat browsers reliably, disclose that limitation; never fabricate a unique-person count from raw sessions.

Follow cohorts of first-time discovery users and people arriving from a shared listing. Measure repeat useful visits, resume behaviour, source error/closure rates, report handling, shared-link landings, task effort, network cost and support burden. A share click is an intent, not delivery; a recipient landing is not proof of a completed application. The full-career north-star metric in the existing growth plan remains for later functionality and must not be retroactively claimed from M0 clicks.

Research after launch follows the existing INV schedule. Use consumer observations to choose the next feature, and independently test institutional buyer/recipient assumptions. A useful free feed does not itself establish B2B revenue. Do not require artificial weekly activity from someone whose next opportunity window is months away.

## 7. Defensibility and business discipline

Source relationships, reliable local coverage, correction history, useful communities, portable career continuity and accepted institutional workflows could accumulate into an advantage. Each needs repeated operational evidence. A richer schema, lack of ads, AI feature or locality label is not an unassailable moat. Incumbents can copy individual features and users may use several platforms.

Counter-positioning is a hypothesis requiring evidence that imitation harms the incumbent's economics; no such conflict is proven here. Complementarity means a useful handoff alongside existing platforms, not immunity from competition or an assumed formal integration. Institutional revenue remains the proposed way to sustain free consumer value, with real review/support and acquisition costs included.

The proposal's historical analogies and unsupported competitor/scam statistics are handled in the [accepted-idea and claims register](../governance/06_DISCOVERY_STRATEGY_TRACEABILITY.md). Market-facing claims require current primary evidence and fair task comparisons.
