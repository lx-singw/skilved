## 1. The company we are building

Skilved is a career platform connecting opportunities, a living evidence record, persistent personal assistance and useful human support across a person's education and working life.

The product promise to test is: **Build your future with progress you can carry, and support for your next step.**

A learner can discover an opportunity, understand it, prepare what is missing, ask for specific help, apply through the appropriate route, and retain useful evidence for the next stage.

Over time that same record can support assessed projects, recognition of prior learning preparation, programme completion, employment transitions and institutional recognition.

The ambition is a complete career product and eventually an international company. A complete first journey is the starting point for that ambition, not the full extent of it.

No profile format, model, agent count, social feed or cloud provider is itself a durable competitive advantage.

## 3. Who receives value first

### Apprentice peer

Already engaged in a programme or looking for the next training opportunity. Needs relevant opportunities, evidence of actual learning, assessments and help with the next transition.

Their first experience should work without institutional integration: select a goal, find or bring a relevant opportunity, produce a useful next-step checklist and save reviewed facts.

### School leaver

May have limited work history and need to understand multiple possible routes. The product must accept schoolwork, responsibilities, volunteering and real projects without inventing employment.

A school leaver is not necessarily under 18. Age-sensitive controls follow the actual user's circumstances rather than a label such as student.

### Learner still at school

Can benefit from public opportunities, pathway explanations and supervised preparation. Private accounts, contact, documents and group participation require an appropriate safety and data process before admission.

The audience stays in the strategy even if a particular private feature must wait for readiness.

### Coordinator or mentor

Can help several people, see permissioned unresolved requests, give feedback and avoid repeatedly collecting the same facts. Their participation improves the experience but does not gate ordinary consumer use.

### Employer, programme operator or assessor

Needs relevant information, reliable provenance and workable receipt of evidence. Acceptance must be demonstrated in an actual review task; a logo on a presentation is insufficient.

## 4. The connected product

The organising journey is:

**Discover → understand → choose → prepare → ask for help → review → apply → follow through → record progress → choose the next step.**

A user can enter midway, bring an external application, pursue more than one goal, or pause. Do not force a linear funnel when their life is not linear.

| Surface | Primary user question | Accumulating value |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | What opportunities or routes are relevant? | Saved interests and source-linked requirements |
| My next steps | What should I do next, and why? | Goals, resolved obstacles and dated tasks |
| My record | What can I show about what I have done? | Reviewed facts, work samples and attributed evidence |
| Opportunity workspace | What is needed for this specific opportunity? | Versioned preparation and application history |
| Help and groups | Who can help me resolve this issue? | Contextual answers, feedback and responsible referrals |
| Programme progress | How do I start, complete and move forward? | Learning, supervision and transition evidence |
| Agent activity | What happened while I was away? | Inspectable actions, sources and unfinished work |

These surfaces share records and events. They should not become disconnected mini-products with competing profile forms.

## 5. What belongs at public launch

All six categories must be real, discoverable and usable for preparation and external application handoff.

The public catalogue must not depend on employers signing up. Users can bring a link for private preparation even when the opportunity is absent from the public catalogue.

A reviewed personal record, application-specific checklist, saved work, truthful status history, useful reminders and export create a complete early product.

The first release does not require national coverage, universal portal submission, a public activity feed, official credential issuance or a government API.

Manual quality review is legitimate operational work. Label it honestly and measure the time it consumes.

The separate coordinator paid pilot can test a buyer and generate revenue while software develops. It is not the sole product, sole acquisition channel or a prerequisite for the public release.

## 6. Release sequence

| Release | Product outcome | Main condition for progression |
|---|---|---|
| R0 — Foundation | Running, inspectable baseline and consistent product/data contracts | Existing mock behaviour and trust claims identified; build and ownership boundaries established |
| R1 — Supervised alpha | Reachable users complete a real opportunity-preparation journey | Observed usability, truthful records, support ownership and recoverable tasks |
| R2 — Public MVP | All six categories, portable record, persistent preparation and public discovery | Source quality, privacy, accessibility, reliability and operating readiness |
| R3 — Groups, partners and evidence | Structured help, small groups, referrals and assessed-project pilots | Real helpers, moderation, recipient usefulness and evidence handling |
| R4 — Institutional continuity | Programme support, institution-accepted records, credential/RPL pathways | Contracted workflows, appropriate assessors/issuers and validated economics |
| R5 — Advanced intelligence and expansion | Supported cohort insights and broader countries/integrations | Representative-enough data for stated claims, local operations and repeatable value |

A release label is a dependency and evidence stage, not proof of implementation or a fixed calendar promise. The [roadmap](../planning/15_BUILD_ROADMAP.md) supplies effort and schedule scenarios.

## 7. Differentiation hypotheses

### Opportunity readiness rather than ranking alone

Users understand source requirements and resolve missing items. Measure preparation correctness and progress against the tools they already use.

### Evidence accumulated through real work

Evidence is linked to an activity, artefact, reviewer and purpose. It becomes reusable without overstating what a reviewer established.

### Assistance that resumes

A saved task survives inactivity and restarts when a relevant fact changes. A chatbot conversation without durable task state does not satisfy this promise.

### Useful human support with context

Someone receives a specific request, can respond or decline, and the person seeking help knows the next state. Measure resolution rather than message volume.

### Continuity after selection

The record remains useful for starting, learning, completion, renewal and the next transition. A successful user need not keep searching for jobs to count as retained value.

These hypotheses need comparison; none is asserted to be unique across the market.

## 8. Potential moats and the work required to earn them

| Potential advantage | Necessary investment | Evidence it is emerging | What does not establish it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trusted institutional use | Delivery, integration, support and accountable permissions | Receiving organisations repeatedly use records in real workflows | Announced interest or unsigned discussions |
| Accepted evidence | Rubrics, credible assessors, provenance and review | Named recipients accept specified evidence for a specified purpose | A blue badge or an uploaded PDF |
| Better obstacle resolution | Clear events, quality review and iterative experiments | Repeatable improvement on defined user tasks | More model calls or an undifferentiated dataset |
| Community usefulness | Reliable helpers, moderation and shared purpose | New relevant members help existing members progress | Registrations, follower counts or feed time |
| Distribution relationships | Continued value to groups, schools and programmes | Repeated voluntary introduction and use | Scraping an organisation's website |
| Reliable operations | Source maintenance, privacy, recovery and cost visibility | Trust survives incidents and routine change | A complex architecture diagram |

Portability remains a user benefit. Do not create a moat by making records difficult to export or by withholding a person's own evidence after departure.

## 9. Learning, assessment and RPL

R3 can support projects with an explicit brief, allowed assistance, submission, rubric, feedback and revision. Label practice, independent assessment and issuer credentials distinctly.

R4 can organise prior-work evidence against the requirements supplied by an appropriate RPL provider, support missing-evidence tasks and record the provider's decision.

Skilved should not claim that AI review grants a qualification or that a digital record guarantees credit. Assessment and recognition belong to the authorised receiving process.

SAQA publishes an RPL implementation policy; use it as research context and confirm the applicable provider and current requirements before implementation. [SAQA policy](https://www.saqa.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Updated-RPL-Policy-website-version-Resized.pdf)

## 10. Interoperability and public infrastructure

Treat MyMzansi, SA Youth, institutions and credential systems as potential interoperable services, not assumed launch dependencies.

Begin with useful exports, permissioned viewing and source references. Add APIs or formal credentials when access, terms and recipient use are confirmed.

The 1EdTech CLR standard is relevant to portable learning and employment records; choosing a standard does not itself confer issuer authority, acceptance or certification. [1EdTech CLR](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/clr)

Every integration should have a value statement, authorised data flow, owner, fallback and testable acceptance criterion.

## 11. Expansion beyond the first cohort

Broader South African coverage expands sources, disciplines, languages and support reach while preserving the shared journey.

Country expansion additionally requires local source rights, programme terminology, qualifications, age/data rules, languages, payment feasibility and support ownership.

A globally accessible website is not equivalent to country-level operational readiness. Advertise actual coverage and allow unknown or unsupported fields rather than mapping everything to SA categories.

Prioritise countries from user demand and a supportable route to service, not a predetermined claim to cover Africa by a particular year.

## 12. Strategy guardrails and decision cadence

At each sprint review, inspect a user journey, source errors, incomplete tasks, support load, cost per useful task and an evidence item from validation.

At each release review, compare observed value with alternatives and decide what to continue, change or stop.

A feature can stay in the long-term vision while its implementation is deferred. A release can move when capacity changes without rewriting history.

The quality objective is excellence on the measures that matter together: usefulness, correctness, accessibility, privacy, reliability, economics and accepted outcomes.

Use the [benchmark protocol](../research/05_COMPETITIVE_BENCHMARKS.md) to prevent isolated wins or artificial demonstrations from becoming claims of market leadership.
