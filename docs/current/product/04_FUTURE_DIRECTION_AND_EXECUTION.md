## 1. The thesis to test

**Skilved helps people turn opportunities into progress they can carry into the next stage of their lives.**

Two complementary mission formulations proposed in the discussion are retained: **help people find opportunities today, build evidence of what they can do, and prepare for what comes next**; and **help people access opportunities, develop their abilities, and carry their progress forward throughout their working lives**. These are proposed expressions of the direction, not a selected final brand line. The immediate M0 task promise remains finding, understanding, saving/sharing and applying at the original source.

The long-term opportunity is attentive, affordable career support: discover a route, understand requirements, prepare, obtain feedback, act, retain evidence and continue. AI may reduce interpretation and preparation effort. Accurate sources, genuine opportunities, accountable judgement, recipient acceptance and distribution still require deliberate work.

The most important hypothesis is that people lose useful progress at transitions between existing services. Observe those transitions before deciding which are most valuable to improve. Skilved should help when a person leaves it to apply, learn, work or receive support elsewhere.

Illustrative later journey, not existing functionality: an apprentice finds a real opportunity, understands a missing requirement, completes relevant preparation, obtains appropriate feedback and selects evidence for the recipient. If the opportunity closes, the useful preparation and evidence survive. A school leaver seeking funding can follow the same broad pattern without pretending their requirements are identical. Users can pursue several routes or pause.

The same continuity should apply after rejection: useful preparation, feedback and truthful evidence can support the next opportunity. This does not imply that every document remains valid indefinitely or that every recipient accepts the same evidence. A polished CV is one output; understanding one's strengths, explaining real work and completing the next task are separate benefits to evaluate.

The first release remains M0-A–G: free discovery across bursaries, learnerships, apprenticeships, internships, graduate programmes and jobs; permitted sourcing and reviewed posting; source-faithful explanations; local progress; sharing; issuer pages and shelves; corrections; and reviewed public-link intake. Both internship routes remain supported. Initial outreach to apprentice peers and school leavers does not restrict the catalogue or wider ambition.

## 2. What the evidence supports

These sources were checked on 24 September 2026. Their findings do not validate Skilved's demand or performance.

- **Earlier skills-change rationale:** employers surveyed for the WEF's 2025 report expected 39% of workers' core skills to change by 2030. This is a forecast from surveyed employers, not an observed future outcome, a South Africa-specific rate or a claim that AI alone causes the change. [WEF 2025 skills outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/3-skills-outlook/)
- **Earlier job-transformation rationale:** the ILO's 2025 update estimates occupational exposure to generative AI and identifies transformation as more likely than full replacement for most exposed jobs. Exposure is not a measured job loss or a guarantee about an individual occupation. [ILO 2025 update](https://www.ilo.org/publications/generative-ai-and-jobs-2025-update)
- **Earlier hiring-change rationale:** LinkedIn's 2025 recruiting report describes AI and skills-based hiring as active industry directions. This provider report is context for competition and experiments, not independent evidence of Skilved's advantage. [LinkedIn 2025 recruiting report](https://business.linkedin.com/hire/resources/future-of-recruiting)

- **Existing local infrastructure:** SA Youth already offers free earning/learning discovery, preparation resources and human assistance. Its service page says access is zero-rated on specified networks. We should understand and potentially complement existing services. This is provider documentation, not an independent impact evaluation. [Harambee work-seeker services](https://www.harambee.co.za/work-seekers/)
- **AI discovery is already competitive:** LinkedIn documents conversational job search available worldwide. Our differentiation must be demonstrated across a useful task, rather than assumed from adding an AI interface. Vendor documentation establishes advertised functionality, not effectiveness. [LinkedIn help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a6889044)
- **Assisted output and learning differ:** a Turkish school mathematics experiment found that unrestricted GPT assistance improved assisted practice but harmed subsequent unaided performance; a designed tutoring approach mitigated the harm. This is one setting using an earlier model, not a universal verdict on tutoring. Later preparation features should evaluate what people can do independently. [Bastani et al., author paper](https://hamsabastani.github.io/education_llm.pdf)
- **The future has multiple plausible paths:** the WEF's January 2026 paper uses scenarios around AI advancement and talent readiness. It supports stress-testing assumptions, not declaring a certain employment forecast. [WEF, 7 January 2026](https://www.weforum.org/publications/four-futures-for-jobs-in-the-new-economy-ai-and-talent-in-2030/)

## 3. Use AI where improvement can be measured

| Application | Practical use | Evidence before expansion |
|---|---|---|
| Source interpretation | Draft structured facts and explanations for review | Material conditions trace to a dated source; uncertainty and conditional rules survive |
| Understanding | Plain explanations and source-specific next steps in M0; later evaluated language assistance | Users correctly explain original requirements and unknowns |
| Preparation | Later, organise approved facts and identify missing items | Fewer omissions and less total rework than the person's usual method |
| Learning | Later, bounded hints, practice and feedback | Demonstrated learning without the assistant supplying the answer |
| Continuing assistance | Later, retain authorised tasks and respond to relevant changes | Reliable restart, cancellation, permissions and truthful action states |
| Institutional coordination | Post-M0 research followed by a validated handover workflow | Recipient acceptance, lower total workload and an actual paying buyer |

Distinguish source facts, user statements, inferences and unknowns. Practice, AI critique, human feedback and formal assessment mean different things. Generated text cannot establish an official qualification, attendance, recipient acceptance or submission receipt.

Users should not need expert prompting. Candidate action wording from the discussion is: explain this requirement; show what information is missing; help me prepare for this step; show the original source; let me correct what you understood; and help me continue where I stopped. Expose only actions that actually work in the released scope. These actions can be ordinary interface controls and do not require adding AI chat to M0.

Clear, fast mobile pages and understandable wording are immediate priorities. Language assistance, voice and other access methods remain future options to test against observed barriers, device/network conditions and faithful meaning. The goal is support for people who currently navigate difficult transitions with little guidance; neither a particular language nor voice capability is promised for launch.

Evaluate representative six-category cases, ambiguous rules, closed listings, source conflicts and malicious page instructions. Compare against a simpler template or non-AI workflow. More reasoning or agents is worthwhile only when measured improvement justifies cost, latency and review effort.

For M0, generate and review shared explanations when source records change. Every visitor need not trigger a new model call. Keep filters, dates, permissions and publication rules deterministic. Public browsing should remain useful during a model outage. Retain a small, inspectable architecture and replace models behind evaluated interfaces rather than training a foundation model or building elaborate agent infrastructure first.

## 4. Prepare for several futures

The following are our planning scenarios, not predictions quoted from the WEF.

| Possible development | Signal to watch | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Assistance becomes cheaper and widely available | Quality and complete cost on our evaluated tasks | Adopt better models where useful; invest in source operations, continuity and acceptance |
| Hiring becomes more automated and generated applications proliferate | Actual employer requirements and recipient behaviour | Support truthful preparation and attributable work; avoid indiscriminate application volume |
| Skills requirements change unevenly | Dated requirements, credible research and user experience | Explain alternatives and test relevant preparation; preserve uncertainty |
| Adoption remains uneven | Actual institutional access and compatibility | Keep human-readable exports and external handoff; do not require universal APIs |
| Entry routes become more constrained | Real category availability and unresolved obstacles | Explore adjacent legitimate routes and capacity partners; never imply training guarantees work |

A monthly, time-bounded review can record signal, evidence, confidence, user consequence and next experiment. No public forecasting feature is required for M0. Future guides must separate forecasts from available opportunities and must not infer national demand from our incomplete catalogue.

Near term: trustworthy discovery and increasingly useful preparation. A conditional 1–3 year ambition: continuity through selected transitions and a repeatable organisational workflow. Longer term: a person-controlled record accepted by multiple recipients, with assistance across institutions and countries. These are investment horizons, not delivery dates. Preserve sources, versioned records and exportable data now; add later private records and interoperability when their full controls and user value are ready.

## 5. Earn lasting advantages

Potential advantages accumulate through accurate maintained sources, accepted evidence, continuity that saves repeated work, voluntary referrals and knowledge of which interventions resolve real obstacles. The model interface and scraped catalogue alone are reproducible.

Compare the complete task with the tools people already use, including local services and general AI assistants. Explain where Skilved helps and where another service does better. A network effect exists when another relevant participant improves someone else's outcome; registrations and scraped organisations do not establish one.

Records remain portable. Continued use should come from service quality rather than withholding a person's evidence. The expansion sequence is a useful individual task, repeatable value at the next event, selected human/recipient participation, a recurring organisational workflow, then wider coverage. Keep all six discovery categories while testing deeper support where users demonstrate need.

## 6. Reach and a sustainable free service

Test three loops separately:

1. **User value:** useful opportunity → understandable action → saved progress → useful return when circumstances change. Measure relevant return, not compulsory daily activity.
2. **Distribution:** voluntary sharing → another person completes a useful action → repeated recommendations. Start with reachable communities and willing administrators. No outreach or partnership is executed by this plan.
3. **Institutional economics:** accepted handover → reduced total rework → organisation pays for a supportable workflow → revenue supports free consumer access. Consumer traffic alone does not validate this chain.

The consumer service remains entirely free. Organisations may fund coordination, programme evidence and reporting after research establishes need, authority and budget. A sponsor does not own the person's whole career record or buy undisclosed influence over guidance. Broader institutional research remains after M0.

Measure monthly cost as fixed services + source collection/review + variable inference + storage/transfer + messaging + support. Record founder labour separately from cash cost. Track cost per completed useful task alongside errors and support load. Sustainable free access needs efficient shared processing and clear service limits; future funding and unlimited inference cannot be assumed.

Funding discussions should present a working demonstration, observed tasks, source operations, costs and the next concrete milestone. Grants, credits and investment are possible funding routes, not secured runway. Weekly capacity and monthly budget remain unresolved, so this document adopts no new price or launch date.

## 7. Make the impact mechanism explicit

Matching applicants to a fixed set of places can improve access and fairness, but does not itself create additional opportunity. Broader impact needs mechanisms such as fewer missed eligible opportunities, less incomplete preparation, more legitimate places filled, less avoidable dropout, stronger demonstrated capability or more capacity for employers and mentors to support people.

Transport, funding, connectivity, accredited training, workplace places and employer demand may need partners, capital or policy. Detecting an obstacle and identifying a genuine support route is useful. Do not mark an obstacle resolved because a model produced advice.

M0 measures: relevant opportunity discovery, requirement comprehension, correct handoff, working save/resume/share, source errors and operator effort. A route click is not an application; a user's report is not recipient confirmation. Unknown outcomes stay unknown.

Later add recipient acceptance, preparation rework, confirmed starts/completions and continuity at the next transition. Use denominators, observation windows, missing outcomes and relevant access differences. Avoid collecting sensitive attributes merely to fill a dashboard. Compare with existing methods; stronger causal claims require a suitable evaluation design. An outcome after Skilved use is not automatically caused by Skilved.

The smallest founder preparation step proposed earlier was weekly capacity, a monthly rand ceiling and six original source links, one per category where currently available. That is a starting research sample; the 2–3 examples per category above deepen it. Neither quantity is the public-launch inventory acceptance threshold, and closed or unavailable categories must not be filled with fabricated listings.

Assign an actual source-maintenance owner and recurring time for reviewing contributions, checking changes/deadlines, correcting broken links and handling reports. The founder may initially take that role; an unassigned role in a document does not establish coverage. Budget source and support work alongside development.

The first response suggested 3–5 potential participants; the later brief recommends 5–8 consenting adults for an initial formative round. This is an evolution of a suggested sample, not two additive studies or a statistically derived requirement. Start with available participants, document their coverage and gaps, and extend when observed questions warrant it. Keep younger audiences in the overall strategy; private or supervised participation follows its own existing readiness requirements.

Working division proposed in the conversation: the founder supplies lived context, access to willing participants, practical priorities and operating constraints; assistant-supported engineering supplies research, implementation, checks, documentation and experiment analysis during authorised work. AI assistance can increase our capacity, but the actual delivery rate must be measured. This division does not appoint a real external team or promise unattended operation, automatic outreach or work between sessions.

Use isolated labelled fixtures for technical work while real sources are reviewed. Public launch needs permitted representative inventory and all exposed M0 acceptance. Full accounts, private documents, AI chat and institution dashboards remain later work.

In observation, ask a person to find a relevant route, explain one material requirement and an uncertainty, identify a next step and reach the correct source. Add save/share trials as those slices exist. Compare with their usual method using similar tasks and counterbalance order where feasible. Record failures and coaching. A small formative study finds problems; it cannot establish population-level impact.

## 9. Continue, change or stop

Before each experiment, record task, sample, comparison, criteria, cost and owner. Numerical thresholds are management choices for that experiment, not industry facts.

- Material source/explanation errors: repair review and narrow generated output before expansion.
- Understanding without action: investigate the obstacle before adding engagement features.
- Little improvement over existing methods: revise the task or experience before adding scope.
- Support cost per useful task exceeds affordable capacity or increases with scale: improve routing/self-service or narrow promises before growth. Some proportionate human review may remain both useful and affordable.
- Recipients reject prepared output: establish accepted requirements before richer evidence features.
- Installed institutional software solves the task: complement it or choose another demonstrated gap.
- Buyer interest lacks budget, authority or repeat use: keep revenue unvalidated and defer speculative dashboards.
- Wider geography weakens source/support quality: stabilise operations before extending coverage.

This brief makes impact, AI evaluation, scenarios and experiment decisions explicit without expanding M0. The next proof is a trustworthy working task observed with real people. Further strategy work should answer uncertainties found in delivery rather than postpone that proof.

## 11. Adopted operational refinements — 24 September

The [adoption register](../governance/08_OPERATIONAL_REFINEMENTS_2026-09-24.md) identifies the accepted subset and implementation homes. It does not adopt the critique wholesale or retrospectively mark every recommendation in this brief founder-confirmed.

Preserve all six categories, both internship routes, broad jobs, free consumer use and the later living record/network/institutional ambition. Build stronger source depth around reachable communities while evaluating actual search usefulness rather than arbitrary listing counts. Explain that local progress can be lost through site-data deletion/device changes and is visible on shared browsers. Offer a reviewed public preparation checklist through preview, copy, plain-text download and supported sharing; it is a static reference, not a backup of personal progress or guaranteed data-free/permanent storage.

Show sourced location, attendance, pay periods and available travel/accommodation support with concise practical prompts. A user-entered affordability comparison is a later experiment; no invented fares, compulsory net-stipend filter or dropout forecast enters M0. Pair unknown requirements with a supported next action without inventing urgency, certification rules, contacts or fees.

Prioritise the actual technical boundary and failing checks from the audit, using dependency-aware scoped execution rather than mandatory wholesale package removal. Measure bytes, source-review effort and total operating costs; reuse reviewed content without per-visitor model calls. Keep institutional software deferred under the existing evidence gates and the commercial hypothesis available for post-M0 investigation, without archiving it or assuming a particular buyer's purchasing speed. Reforecast affected B slices; no implementation or unchanged estimate is implied.
