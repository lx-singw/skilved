## What Is Skilved

Skilved (Skilled + Verified) is the AI advancement layer built on South Africa's digital public infrastructure. It answers the question MyMzansi cannot: **what should this person do next?**

The MVP is a continuously running AI agent that discovers every skilled trades opportunity in South Africa, matches each opportunity to the right worker, and helps them apply — with zero signup required.

**"Not just skilled. Skilved."**

## The Strategy in One Page

### The Secret (Thiel)
A verified government identity is economically worthless without a verified economic opportunity layer. MyMzansi is building the first for 60 million South Africans. Nobody is building the second.

### The Moat (Cirlig)
The outcome graph. After 3 years of data — which learnerships led to jobs, which qualifications unlock which salary ranges, which career paths are real — no competitor can replicate it cold. The graph compounds with every user.

### The Architecture
- Government builds layers 1–3: identity, credentials, payments (MyMzansi)
- Skilved builds layer 4: opportunity intelligence — what should this person do next?

### The MVP (Thiel's books)
All SA trades, all provinces. AI discovers every apprenticeship, learnership, bursary, and artisan job every 4 hours. Workers get matched without searching. No signup required. No gates.

### The XPRIZE Play
Entrepreneurship & Job Creation category. Real users (trades workers — acute pain). Real revenue (employer referral fees from Day 14). Genuine AI-native operations (5 agents, zero human curation). Category impact story that spans 90 days and 10 years.

### The 10-Year Vision
Skilved becomes the advancement layer of South Africa's digital economy — then Africa's. The intelligence that tells 800 million working-age Africans what they should do next, anchored to their government-verified identity.
