## What Is Skilved

Skilved (Skilled + Verified) is South Africa's portable verified economic identity — built on MyMzansi digital public infrastructure rails — that tells every institution not just who you are, but what you can do, what you've done, and what you should do next.

Starting with trade skills — the most acute need, the clearest pain, the fastest path to verifiable outcomes. **Trades is the entry point, not the ceiling.** The architecture serves every South African worker at every qualification level — from matric to PhD.

**Nineteen AI employees** work 24 hours a day on behalf of every worker who builds a Skills Passport: discovering opportunities, matching them by real fit, verifying documents, mapping career paths, submitting applications autonomously with complete verified documents, coordinating interviews, and building the SA Skills Graph — while the worker sleeps.

The Document Vault means workers upload their ID, qualifications, and certificates once — and share them forever, with explicit consent, to any institution that needs them. Never upload the same document twice.

The Skills Passport is the moat. The graph is the compound interest. The Document Vault is the portable identity. The intelligence layer (Skills Pulse, Career Simulation, Employer Accountability, Collective Intelligence) brings an awesome future to the present.

**"Not just skilled. Skilved."**

**The sentence that defines the category:**
> "LinkedIn helps people present themselves. Skilved helps opportunities find people — with a verified identity they carry forever."

**The sentence that wins first place at XPRIZE:**
> "We didn't optimise the existing system. We changed what information is available, to whom, and when — giving South African workers a portable verified economic identity built on government infrastructure, for the first time."

## The Strategy in One Page

### The Core Reframe
**Current world:** Humans hunt opportunities, re-upload the same documents to every institution.
**Skilved world:** Opportunities hunt humans — through agents. Documents live in one place, shared with consent.

### The Secret (Thiel)
A verified government identity (MyMzansi) is economically worthless without a verified economic opportunity layer. Nobody is building the intelligence layer on top of it — or the portable identity vault that makes it usable for employment. Skilved does both.

### The Moat (Cirlig)
The outcome graph + the document vault. After 3 years: which learnerships led to jobs, which qualifications unlock which salary ranges, which career paths are real — nobody can replicate this cold. And every worker who uploaded their documents once means Skilved holds the verified identity infrastructure for SA's working class.

### The Five Pillars (v6.0)

**1. Skills Passport** — AI-built, verified, portable career identity. Not a form you fill in — an agent builds it from your CV, certificates, and work history.

**2. Document Vault** — Upload once, share forever. ID, qualifications, licences, transcripts — all encrypted, verified against SAQA/NAMB/MyMzansi, shareable to any institution with explicit consent. Never upload the same document twice.

**3. Autonomous Application Agent** — Applies on your behalf via 8 ATS adapters (SuccessFactors, Oracle Taleo, PageUp, SETA portals, email). Pre-flight checks ensure every application is complete. Review-before-submit ensures you see what was submitted. CAPTCHA solver means nothing blocks the submission.

**4. Intelligence Layer** — Skills Pulse Dashboard (national intelligence), Career Simulation ("what if I get my trade test?"), Employer Accountability (who actually delivers), Collective Intelligence ("what are people like me doing?"). All free. All public. All bringing an awesome future to the present.

**5. MyMzansi Foundation** — Built around SA's Digital Public Infrastructure from day one. "Sign in with MyMzansi." Credentials wallet integration. Government-grade verified identity. Stipend disbursement via MyMzansi payments rail. Skilved completes MyMzansi's social promise.

### The Scope (Updated v6.0)
Trades is the entry point, not the ceiling. The architecture is qualification-agnostic. The same agents, the same passport, the same document vault serve a matric learnerships candidate and a computer science graduate equally. Trades has the highest pain, the clearest SETA infrastructure, and the most verifiable outcomes — so we start there. Phase 2 expands to all TVET qualifications and Grade 12 learnerships. Phase 3 expands to university graduates and professional licences.

### The Architecture
- Government builds layers 1–3: identity, credentials, payments (MyMzansi)
- Skilved builds layer 4: opportunity intelligence + portable identity vault
- Skilved builds layer 5: outcome intelligence + national skills intelligence

### The 19 AI Employees
Skills Profile → Scout (+ TVET colleges) → Analyst (+ required docs) → Quality → Matching → Career → Application (+ pre-flight + review) → Revenue → Growth → Customer Success + Skills Pulse + Career Simulation + Employer Accountability + Collective Intelligence + **Document Verification** + **Scope Expansion** + Notification + Outcome Tracker

Zero human approvals. Zero incomplete applications submitted. Zero documents re-uploaded.
