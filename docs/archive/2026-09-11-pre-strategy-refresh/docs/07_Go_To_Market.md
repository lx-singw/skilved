## GTM Philosophy

Skilved has three customers: workers (free, never pay), employers/SETAs (pay for access to matched candidates), and government (pay for intelligence). The GTM strategy must run all three tracks simultaneously from day one.

**Worker acquisition:** organic, viral, WhatsApp-first, community-led
**Employer/SETA acquisition:** direct outreach, referral fees, institutional contracts
**Government acquisition:** Skills Pulse Dashboard as the lead generation tool — show them the intelligence, then convert to paid license

The feed is the acquisition engine for workers. Every share is a free impression.
The Skills Pulse Dashboard is the acquisition engine for government. Every official who bookmarks it is a future procurement relationship.
The Employer Accountability Layer is the acquisition engine for quality employers. A-rated employers have reason to promote their score.

## Worker Acquisition Strategy

### Phase 1: TVET College Partnerships (Week 1–4)

TVET colleges are the highest-concentration point of motivated, qualified trades candidates. They actively need placement services for their graduates.

**Target:** 20 TVET colleges with strong trade programmes  
**Pitch:** "Free placement tool for your graduates. We find them opportunities. You track their outcomes. Completely free."  
**What we need from them:** Permission to share Skilved with students, college logo on site (social proof), contact details of placement officer  
**What they get:** Free placement tracking dashboard, outcomes data for their reports, their graduates get jobs  

**Approach:**
1. Email the 20 largest TVET colleges' placement offices (Week 1)
2. Follow up by phone (Week 1–2)
3. On-site visit for first 3 colleges willing to meet (Week 2–3)
4. Announce partnerships on social (Week 3–4)

**Target:** 5 TVET college partnerships signed by Day 30

**Target:** 20 TVET colleges with strong trade programmes
**Pitch:** "Free placement tool for your graduates. We track their outcomes. Completely free."

**NEW v2.0 pitch addition:** "Your college will appear in the TVET College Performance table on our Skills Pulse Dashboard — visible to employers, SETAs, and DHET. Good placement rates attract more learners."

This creates a new incentive for TVET colleges: the dashboard gives them public visibility on their performance — which they want if it's good.

### Phase 2: WhatsApp Community Seeding (Week 1 ongoing)

The target user is already in WhatsApp groups. Our job is to get into those groups organically.

**Trade WhatsApp groups to target:**
- "Electrical Apprentices SA" type groups (search Facebook for these, find admins)
- MERSETA/EWSETA applicant groups
- TVET student groups (via college partnerships)
- Trade union WhatsApp channels (NUMSA, BCAWU, SAEWA)
- Reddit r/southafrica (job/skills threads)

**Content strategy for WhatsApp:**
- Share 1–2 high-quality opportunities directly into groups per week (with group admin permission)
- Pre-formatted message: trade, location, salary, deadline, link
- Not spam — genuinely useful content that earns its place

**Organic viral trigger:**
When the feed goes live with 200+ real opportunities, the first natural shares will happen. Our job is to make sharing as easy as one tap (WhatsApp share button on every card).

**NEW v2.0 content for WhatsApp groups:**
Beyond opportunity sharing, share Skills Pulse insights:
> "📊 Skilved Skills Intelligence: Critical shortage of electricians in Limpopo right now — 47 open opportunities, only 12 qualified candidates applying. If you're in Limpopo, this is your moment: [link to feed]"

This type of content gets shared beyond just the original recipient — it's genuinely useful trade community intelligence.

### Phase 3: SEO (Week 2 ongoing)

Every trade + province combination is a high-intent search query with almost no good current answers.

- "electrical apprenticeship gauteng 2026" — currently terrible results
- "welding learnership south africa" — job boards with out-of-date listings
- "MERSETA learnership 2026" — SETA's own site, hard to navigate

Skilved's structured data and fresh content will rank within 30–60 days for these terms. This is free, compounding acquisition.

**Priority keyword clusters:**
1. [Trade] + apprenticeship/learnership + [Province]
2. SETA + [year] learnership
3. Artisan jobs [Province]
4. Trade test registration SA

**NEW v2.0 keyword additions:**
- "best learnership employers South Africa [year]" — Employer Accountability Layer drives this
- "SA artisan shortage [province]" — Skills Pulse Dashboard drives this
- "N4 electrical salary South Africa" — Career Simulation drives this
- "TVET college placement rates South Africa" — Skills Pulse TVET table drives this

### Phase 4: Social Media (Week 3 ongoing)

**Platform priority:** TikTok + Instagram (Reels) > Facebook > LinkedIn

**Content strategy:**
- "Skilved found this R5,000/month electrical apprenticeship in Gauteng — here's how to apply"
- Behind-the-scenes: "How our AI found 847 new opportunities this week"
- User stories: "Thandeka applied through Skilved and started her apprenticeship"
- SETA explainer series: "What is a learnership and how do you apply?"

**Creator partnerships:**
Find 5–10 small SA trades content creators (TikTok, YouTube) who talk about their trade journey. Offer them first access to Skilved and ask for authentic reviews. Not paid partnerships — genuine product placement.

**NEW v2.0 content angles:**
- Skills Pulse data as shareable social content: "📊 This week: Limpopo has a critical electrician shortage. 47 jobs open, 12 qualified candidates. If you're N3 Electrical, this is your moment."
- Career Simulation results as social proof: "Skilved told me getting my trade test would add R3,900/month to my salary. It was right."
- Employer Accountability surprise: "Only 4 employers in SA have a Skilved Accountability Score of A+. Here they are."

### Phase 5: Skills Pulse as Media Asset — NEW v2.0

The Skills Pulse Dashboard creates earned media opportunities that no job board has ever had:

**Target publications:**
- Daily Maverick (labour market stories)
- Business Day (skills development stories)
- SABC/eNCA (trade skills shortage stories)
- Engineering News (artisan shortage coverage)
- Department newsletters (DHET quarterly)

**Pitch:** "Skilved has the first real-time national skills intelligence platform. We update daily. Our data shows [specific insight from dashboard]. Would you like to interview our data?"

**Expected outcome:** Regular citations in national media. Each citation drives awareness among both workers and employers. Eventually, DHET officials start quoting Skilved data in parliamentary questions — the ultimate credibility signal.

### Phase 6: Radio + Community (Month 2–3)
*(Unchanged from v1.0)*

**NEW v2.0 addition:** "Skills Shortage of the Week" segment — agent-generated weekly insight from Skills Pulse Dashboard, formatted for radio presentation.

## Employer / SETA Acquisition Strategy

### Week 1–2: Direct Outreach Campaign

**Target list:** 50 mid-size trade employers in Gauteng (start where most users are)

**Outreach script (email):**

> Subject: [X] qualified electrical candidates in Gauteng looking for apprenticeships right now

> Hi [Name],
>
> Skilved's AI agent has indexed your sector and found [X] active apprenticeship seekers in Gauteng with [relevant qualifications].
>
> We're launching a new performance-based placement service: you pay R[amount] per qualified candidate we refer to you. No placement, no charge. No advertising required.

> We have [number] qualified [trade] candidates in your area right now.
>
> 15 minutes to show you the pipeline?
>
> [Founder name]
> Skilved

**Follow-up:** Phone call Day 3. Demo on Day 5 if interested. Contract Week 2.

**Pricing for early employers:**
- First 3 referrals free (no-risk trial)
- R500 per referral after (introductory rate)
- R1,000 per referral from Month 2

**NEW v2.0 outreach angle for employers with good track records:**

> Subject: Your learnership programme has a strong track record — we've verified it
>
> Hi [Name],

> Skilved has verified [X] placements from your [trade] learnership programme.
> Your accountability score: A (94/100).
>
> We're launching our public employer intelligence layer where workers can see verified
> employer scores before applying. This would give [Company] a significant competitive
> advantage in attracting qualified candidates.
>
> Want to feature your score on the platform before we go live?
>
> [Founder name]

This creates a first-mover incentive for good employers.

### SETA Outreach (Month 1–2)

SETAs are the highest-value institutional customers. Each SETA manages learnerships worth millions of rand. They need qualified candidates.

**Target first:** MERSETA, EWSETA, CETA (largest trade-focused SETAs)

**Pitch:**
> "Skilved is indexing your learnership opportunities and making them visible to the most qualified candidates in SA. We'd like to discuss a formal partnership where we handle candidate discovery and pre-qualification for your discretionary learnership grants."

**SETA partnership structure:**
- Skilved surfaces their learnerships on the feed (free, already happening)
- SETA pays for candidate pipeline access (R5,000–R20,000/year)
- Optional: Skilved manages their learnership application portal

**Government procurement:** Most SETAs require formal procurement processes. Start relationship-building now. Revenue from SETAs in Month 2–3.

**NEW v2.0 SETA pitch addition:** Show SETAs their own performance in the Skills Pulse Dashboard before the meeting. "We've already calculated the placement rate for your learnerships based on verified outcomes. Here's what it shows — and here's what it shows about your competitors."

SETAs are competitive. Showing them their performance vs. other SETAs is a powerful hook.

### Enterprise Employer Strategy (Month 3+)

Eskom, Transnet, Sasol, Anglo American — all have large apprenticeship and learnership programmes that are currently managed manually.

**Sales motion:**
- Reach via existing SETA relationships (SETAs have employer relationships)
- Attend SETA sector skills committee meetings (public, open to stakeholders)
- Reference first mid-size employer wins as case studies

**Enterprise pricing:**
- Dashboard access: R5,000/month
- Unlimited candidate referrals: R20,000/month
- Full integration (ATS API): R50,000 setup + R10,000/month

## Intelligence Layer GTM — NEW v2.0

### Skills Pulse Dashboard Distribution

**Free tier (immediate):**
- Public dashboard at `skilved.com/skills-pulse` — no login
- Monthly PDF report downloadable — no login
- Social sharing buttons on all key stats

**Distribution channels:**
1. Email to every SETA CEO/COO (show them their sector's data)
2. Email to DHET's Skills Development Unit
3. Email to 9 provincial skills offices
4. Post monthly insights on LinkedIn (reach government officials)
5. Submit to DPSA newsletter
6. Pitch to Daily Maverick/Business Day as data source

**Conversion path:**
- Free dashboard → relationship built → government official bookmarks
- Government official references in a report
- Government official requests bespoke analysis
- Paid data license (R200K–R1M/year)

**Target:** First government conversation Month 2. First paid government license Month 6.

### Employer Accountability Layer Distribution

**Free tier (immediate):**
- All employer scores publicly visible on opportunity cards
- Employer profiles at `skilved.com/employer/[slug]` — public

**Conversion path for good employers:**
- Show employer their A score (they didn't ask for it — it's just there)
- Offer to feature it prominently (paid: R2,000–R8,000/month)
- Good employers promote their Skilved score to candidates

**Conversion path for bad employers:**
- Show employer their D/F score
- Offer accountability improvement programme (not just fixing the score — actually improving learnership quality, with Skilved tracking)
- Premium service: "Accountability Improvement Plan" — consulting-led, tech-backed

### Career Simulation Engine Distribution

**Free tier (immediate):**
- Available to all authenticated users (passport ≥ 40%)
- One simulation free per day

**Conversion path:**
- User runs simulation: "Getting trade test = +R3,900/month"
- Premium Level 3 prompt: "Let me apply to trade test opportunities automatically"
- Worker upgrades to Premium

**B2B distribution (Phase 3):**
- White-label for TVET colleges: "Powered by Skilved Career Intelligence"
- White-label for SETAs: show candidates the career impact of their learnership
- Pitch to corporate L&D as workforce planning tool
