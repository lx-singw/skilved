## GTM Philosophy

Skilved has three customers: workers (free, never pay), employers/SETAs (pay for access to matched candidates), and government (pay for intelligence). The GTM strategy must run all three tracks simultaneously from day one.

**Worker acquisition:** organic, viral, WhatsApp-first, community-led
**Employer/SETA acquisition:** direct outreach, referral fees, institutional contracts
**Government acquisition:** Skills Pulse Dashboard as the lead generation tool — show them the intelligence, then convert to paid license

The feed is the acquisition engine for workers. Every share is a free impression.
The Skills Pulse Dashboard is the acquisition engine for government. Every official who bookmarks it is a future procurement relationship.
The Employer Accountability Layer is the acquisition engine for quality employers. A-rated employers have reason to promote their score.

## Worker Acquisition Strategy

### Phase 1: TVET College Partnerships (Week 1–4)
*(Unchanged from v1.0)*

**Target:** 20 TVET colleges with strong trade programmes
**Pitch:** "Free placement tool for your graduates. We track their outcomes. Completely free."

**NEW v2.0 pitch addition:** "Your college will appear in the TVET College Performance table on our Skills Pulse Dashboard — visible to employers, SETAs, and DHET. Good placement rates attract more learners."

This creates a new incentive for TVET colleges: the dashboard gives them public visibility on their performance — which they want if it's good.

### Phase 2: WhatsApp Community Seeding (Week 1 ongoing)
*(Unchanged from v1.0)*

**NEW v2.0 content for WhatsApp groups:**
Beyond opportunity sharing, share Skills Pulse insights:
> "📊 Skilved Skills Intelligence: Critical shortage of electricians in Limpopo right now — 47 open opportunities, only 12 qualified candidates applying. If you're in Limpopo, this is your moment: [link to feed]"

This type of content gets shared beyond just the original recipient — it's genuinely useful trade community intelligence.

### Phase 3: SEO (Week 2 ongoing)
*(Unchanged from v1.0 base)*

**NEW v2.0 keyword additions:**
- "best learnership employers South Africa [year]" — Employer Accountability Layer drives this
- "SA artisan shortage [province]" — Skills Pulse Dashboard drives this
- "N4 electrical salary South Africa" — Career Simulation drives this
- "TVET college placement rates South Africa" — Skills Pulse TVET table drives this

### Phase 4: Social Media (Week 3 ongoing)
*(Unchanged from v1.0 base)*

**NEW v2.0 content angles:**
- Skills Pulse data as shareable social content: "📊 This week: Limpopo has a critical electrician shortage. 47 jobs open, 12 qualified candidates. If you're N3 Electrical, this is your moment."
- Career Simulation results as social proof: "Skilved told me getting my trade test would add R3,900/month to my salary. It was right."
- Employer Accountability surprise: "Only 4 employers in SA have a Skilved Accountability Score of A+. Here they are."

### Phase 5: Skills Pulse as Media Asset — NEW v2.0

The Skills Pulse Dashboard creates earned media opportunities that no job board has ever had:

**Target publications:**
- Daily Maverick (labour market stories)
- Business Day (skills development stories)
- SABC/eNCA (trade skills shortage stories)
- Engineering News (artisan shortage coverage)
- Department newsletters (DHET quarterly)

**Pitch:** "Skilved has the first real-time national skills intelligence platform. We update daily. Our data shows [specific insight from dashboard]. Would you like to interview our data?"

**Expected outcome:** Regular citations in national media. Each citation drives awareness among both workers and employers. Eventually, DHET officials start quoting Skilved data in parliamentary questions — the ultimate credibility signal.

### Phase 6: Radio + Community (Month 2–3)
*(Unchanged from v1.0)*

**NEW v2.0 addition:** "Skills Shortage of the Week" segment — agent-generated weekly insight from Skills Pulse Dashboard, formatted for radio presentation.

## Intelligence Layer GTM — NEW v2.0

### Skills Pulse Dashboard Distribution

**Free tier (immediate):**
- Public dashboard at `skilved.com/skills-pulse` — no login
- Monthly PDF report downloadable — no login
- Social sharing buttons on all key stats

**Distribution channels:**
1. Email to every SETA CEO/COO (show them their sector's data)
2. Email to DHET's Skills Development Unit
3. Email to 9 provincial skills offices
4. Post monthly insights on LinkedIn (reach government officials)
5. Submit to DPSA newsletter
6. Pitch to Daily Maverick/Business Day as data source

**Conversion path:**
- Free dashboard → relationship built → government official bookmarks
- Government official references in a report
- Government official requests bespoke analysis
- Paid data license (R200K–R1M/year)

**Target:** First government conversation Month 2. First paid government license Month 6.

### Employer Accountability Layer Distribution

**Free tier (immediate):**
- All employer scores publicly visible on opportunity cards
- Employer profiles at `skilved.com/employer/[slug]` — public

**Conversion path for good employers:**
- Show employer their A score (they didn't ask for it — it's just there)
- Offer to feature it prominently (paid: R2,000–R8,000/month)
- Good employers promote their Skilved score to candidates

**Conversion path for bad employers:**
- Show employer their D/F score
- Offer accountability improvement programme (not just fixing the score — actually improving learnership quality, with Skilved tracking)
- Premium service: "Accountability Improvement Plan" — consulting-led, tech-backed

### Career Simulation Engine Distribution

**Free tier (immediate):**
- Available to all authenticated users (passport ≥ 40%)
- One simulation free per day

**Conversion path:**
- User runs simulation: "Getting trade test = +R3,900/month"
- Premium Level 3 prompt: "Let me apply to trade test opportunities automatically"
- Worker upgrades to Premium

**B2B distribution (Phase 3):**
- White-label for TVET colleges: "Powered by Skilved Career Intelligence"
- White-label for SETAs: show candidates the career impact of their learnership
- Pitch to corporate L&D as workforce planning tool
