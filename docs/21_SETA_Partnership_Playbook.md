## Why SETAs Are Critical

SETAs (Sector Education and Training Authorities) are the most important institutional customers Skilved will acquire. They:

- Control R25 billion in annual skills development funding
- Fund thousands of learnerships that currently go unfilled
- Have government mandates to place candidates
- Are already crawled by Skilved's discovery agent (free distribution)
- Can be converted from free to paying customers with the right partnership structure

**The SETA flywheel:**
> Skilved indexes SETA learnerships → workers apply → SETA sees better candidate quality → SETA pays for candidate pipeline access → more workers join Skilved → graph improves → SETA sees even better results

## SETA Outreach Strategy

### Phase 1: Passive Value First (Months 1–2)

Before any outreach, Skilved's discovery agent is already indexing SETA learnerships and sending pre-qualified candidates to their application pages. This is the relationship foundation.

**What Skilved does before first contact:**
- Indexes all SETA learnerships automatically
- Sends candidates to SETA application pages
- Builds up referral data (how many candidates Skilved sent this SETA)

**The opening message (email to CEO/COO):**

> Subject: We've already sent [X] qualified candidates to your learnerships
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Skilved is a new AI-powered platform for South African skilled trades workers. In the past [X] weeks, our agents have indexed [N] MERSETA learnerships and directed [X] pre-qualified candidates to your application pages.
>
> We'd like to formalise this and give MERSETA direct insight into the candidate pipeline we're building — including qualification levels, provinces, and application outcomes.
>
> 20 minutes to show you the data?
>
> [Name]  
> Founder, Skilved  
> skilved.com

**Why lead with data:** Most SETA outreach asks SETAs for something. Skilved leads with something it has already given them (free candidate flow) and offers to show them the data. This is a fundamentally different conversation.

### Phase 2: First Meeting

**Agenda (20 minutes):**
1. Show SETA their learnerships live on Skilved feed (2 min)
2. Show candidate pipeline data (who clicked, what qualifications, what province) (5 min)
3. Show outcome graph (first data points — which of their learnerships got the most qualified candidates) (5 min)
4. Propose partnership (5 min)
5. Next steps (3 min)

**The partnership proposal:**

> Option 1 — Free: We continue indexing your learnerships, you get free candidate flow, no contract needed.
>
> Option 2 — R10,000/year: We provide monthly candidate pipeline reports, pre-qualified candidate profiles, and priority placement tracking.
>
> Option 3 — R50,000/year: Full managed service — we handle candidate screening, shortlisting, and placement tracking for your discretionary learnership grants.

Start with Option 2 for most SETAs. Go straight to Option 3 for MERSETA and EWSETA (largest budgets, most organised).

### Phase 3: Procurement Navigation

SETAs are public entities. Procurement must comply with their SCM policies.

**Key thresholds (check per SETA — varies):**
- Below R500,000: Usually quotation process (3 quotes)
- Below R200,000: Often just approval from CFO
- Below R100,000: Often single-source approval

**Strategy:** Target contracts below R100,000 for first-year engagements. Build track record, then go for larger contracts in Year 2.

**Required documents:**
- Skilved (Pty) Ltd registration certificate
- Tax clearance certificate (SARS)
- B-BBEE certificate or sworn affidavit (EME)
- Bank confirmation letter
- Company profile (1 page)

**Timeline:** From first meeting to signed contract: 30–90 days for most SETAs.

## SETA Partnership Tiers

### Tier 1: Listing Partnership (Free)
- Skilved indexes SETA learnerships automatically
- SETA gets free candidate flow
- No contract, no payment
- SETA logo on Skilved website ("Powered by SETA data")

**Who:** All 21 SETAs — happens automatically via discovery agent

### Tier 2: Candidate Pipeline (R10,000–R30,000/year)
- Monthly candidate pipeline report (PDF):
  - How many candidates viewed each learnership
  - Qualification levels of candidates
  - Provinces of candidates
  - Application click-through rates
- Early access to new Skilved features
- SETA can post learnerships directly (no scraping lag)

**Who:** Priority Tier 1 SETAs (MERSETA, EWSETA, CETA, MQA, TETA, MICT SETA)

### Tier 3: Managed Placement (R50,000–R200,000/year)
- Everything in Tier 2, plus:
- Pre-qualified shortlist per learnership (top 10 candidates ranked by match score)
- Direct candidate contact (Skilved introduces candidates to SETA)
- Placement tracking (Skilved follows up on outcomes)
- DHET compliance report data (SETA uses for annual report)
- Dedicated account manager

**Who:** MERSETA, EWSETA, CETA (largest, most organised, most budget)

### Tier 4: Full SETA OS (R200,000–R500,000/year)
- Everything in Tier 3, plus:
- SETA management portal (Skilved hosts their learnership application system)
- Candidate management workflow (apply → screen → interview → place)
- Stipend tracking integration
- Full DHET reporting suite
- API access for SETA's own systems

**Who:** Phase 2 product. Available Month 9+. Target: 3 large SETAs by Year 2.

## SETA Objection Handling

**"We already have a placement service."**
> "We integrate with your existing process — we don't replace it. Skilved finds you more and better-qualified candidates. Your placement team still does the selection."

**"Our candidates don't use apps / technology."**
> "Skilved works on WhatsApp. 97% of South African adults use WhatsApp. Your candidates are already there."

**"We can't share candidate data."**
> "You don't need to. Skilved generates its own candidate base — we don't ask for your data. We bring new candidates to you."

**"Our procurement process takes 6 months."**
> "Understood. We can start on a free basis immediately — no procurement required. The formal contract follows when procurement is ready."

**"What if candidates you send us are unqualified?"**
> "Our AI matches on qualification level before any referral. If a referred candidate doesn't meet your minimum qualifications, we refund the referral fee. No risk to SETA."
